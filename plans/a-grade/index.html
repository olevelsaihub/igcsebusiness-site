Before you start: supporting skills

These starting skills support many types. Check them first, and come back to them whenever a type feels hard.

  • Reading a business case: Read a short business case and pick out the facts, figures and options a question needs
  • Answering command words: Use the technique cards to answer multiple choice, Define, State, Calculate, Outline, Explain, Analyse, Justify and Evaluate questions
  • Percentages, rounding and money: Work with percentages, decimals, rounding to two places and currency units with a calculator
  • Reading tables, charts and figures: Read values from tables, charts and financial extracts and say what they show
