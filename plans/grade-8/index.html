A-Grade Plan · 48 types

Percentages and change: Change between two values, absolute or percentage · Find a percentage of an amount · Increase or decrease a value by a percentage

Business activity, objectives, stakeholders and ownership: Meaning of stakeholder and shareholder · Primary, secondary and tertiary sectors · Business objectives: financial, non-financial and social · Forms of business ownership and limited liability · Measuring business success

Location, going global, currencies and interest rates: Convert prices between currencies with exchange rates · Effects of interest rate changes · Choosing where to locate a business · Multinationals and globalisation

Departments, employment types and communication: Functional areas and the human resources role · Communication: methods, barriers and why it matters · Types of employment: full-time, part-time, temporary

Recruiting and training staff: Recruitment documents: job description to CV · Internal and external recruitment · Training: induction, on-the-job and off-the-job

Motivating staff: Why motivating and keeping staff matters · Financial and non-financial methods of motivation

Costs, revenue and profit: Fixed and variable costs · Total cost from fixed and variable costs · Total revenue or total pay: rate times quantity · Revenue and profit: meaning and how to raise them · Work out profit or loss

Break-even output, charts and usefulness: Break-even output using contribution · Break-even: meaning, charts and usefulness

Markup, margins and the income statement: Calculate the gross profit margin · Markup: percentage, selling price or cost · Using income statements and financial documents to decide

Sources of finance and cash flow: Why cash matters and how to improve cash flow · Cash-flow forecasts: terms, uses and limits · Net cash flow and closing balance · Sources of finance and why businesses need finance

Marketing and market research: Market research methods: primary and secondary · Why businesses carry out market research · Why marketing matters: needs, loyalty and share

Product life cycle, pricing, promotion and mix choices: Product life cycle and extension strategies · Promotion methods: above and below the line · Technology in promotion and social media · Pricing strategies and when to use them · New products and marketing mix choices

Production methods and scale: Capital-intensive and labour-intensive production · Economies and diseconomies of scale · Production methods: job, batch and flow

Lean production, technology and quality: Technology and robotics in production · Lean production: JIT, Kaizen and using resources well · Quality, quality control and competitive advantage
