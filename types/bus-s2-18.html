Knowledge card

Health and safety at work

Written

Health and safety laws make a business protect staff and customers from harm. Training, safety equipment and regular checks cut accidents and help the business avoid fines and a damaged reputation.

Wet floor, no sign

Hazard
Someone could slip and be injured.
Fix
Put up a warning sign and clean the floor.

Unchecked equipment

Hazard

Fix
Check equipment on a regular timetable.

No safety induction

Hazard
The trainer may not know what to do in an emergency.
Fix
Give safety training at induction.

Find the two results that show why complying pays off.

  1. Fewer accidents
  2. Fewer days off
  3. No fines

Fewer accidents

Effect
Fewer staff are hurt, so work carries on.
Write it as
Training means staff know the risks, so there are fewer accidents.

Fewer days off

Effect
Less sick leave means more output and lower cover costs.

No fines

Effect
Breaking the law can mean fines, being sued or closure.
Trap

4

The method

  1. Name an action

    Pick one: training, equipment checks, protective clothing or following the law.

  2. Link to the workplace

    Tie it to a real hazard in this business, such as hot ovens or heavy weights.

  3. Explain the result

    Say what changes: fewer accidents, or staff who know what to do.

  4. End on the business

    Finish with fines avoided, fewer absences or a protected reputation.

Do not lose marks here

  • ✗
    Stopping at "keeps people safe"

    This needs a development to earn the extra marks.

  • ✗
    Generic rules

    Use hazards from the named business, not safety in general.

  • ✗
    A list of measures

    Explain one measure rather than naming four.

  • ✗
    Missing the law

    Fines and being sued are real business costs. Mention them.

Worked explanation

  1. Explain card: one reason plus two linked expansions.
  2. Reason: staff learn the hazards of their workplace before starting work.
  3. Expansion 1: they know how to use equipment safely and what to do in an emergency.
  4. Expansion 2: fewer accidents, so fewer absences and less risk of fines or legal action.
Model answer · written by olevels.com, not an official Pearson mark scheme

Health and safety training at induction teaches new employees about the dangers in their workplace before they start work. They learn how to use equipment correctly, where the emergency exits are and what to do if there is an accident. This reduces the chance of injuries, so fewer staff are off work, and the business is less likely to be fined or taken to court for breaking health and safety law.

How the marks are earned

1 mark for the reason (learning the hazards before starting). 1 mark for explaining what they learn (safe use of equipment, emergency procedures). 1 mark for the consequence: fewer accidents and lower legal risk.
