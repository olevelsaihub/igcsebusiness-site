One part as a percentage of a total

Expressing one part as a percentage of a whole, such as market share or one category's share of a total.

Type 5 Calculations BUS-CALC-09

★☆☆☆☆ · 3 of 32 eligible examination papers, 2019–2025. Difficulty: Easy.

Asks for the percentage of a total, a share of revenue, or market share for one business or one place.

Knowledge

  • Percentage share = part ÷ total × 100.
  • Market share is one business's sales as a percentage of the total sales of all businesses in the market.
  • If the total is not given, add up all the parts first.
  • Both figures must be in the same unit (for example both in £ millions).
  • Give the answer to the decimal places asked, usually 2.

Before you begin

  • Converting a fraction to a percentage
  • Adding a list of numbers
  • Meaning of market share

Answer technique: Set out a calculation with working (2 marks) · Answer a multiple-choice question

Method

  1. Find the part you need (one business or one country). Why: Wrong options use another business's figure.
  2. Find the total, adding all the parts if it is not given. Why: The share must be out of the whole.
  3. Divide part by total and multiply by 100. Why: This turns the fraction into a percentage.
  4. Round as asked and add the % sign. Why: Rounding is part of the second mark.

How each command word is answered

Command wordTimes askedWhat to write
multiple-choice1Work out the share for the business named; the other options are other businesses' shares. Use the Multiple-choice card.
Calculate2Use the Calculate card: show the total if you added it, then part ÷ total × 100 and the percentage to 2 decimal places.

Common mistakes

Inferred, not measured: Inferred from the official schemes' credit and rejection patterns; not measured student-error statistics.

  • Using the figure for the wrong business or location.
  • Dividing the total by the part instead of the part by the total.
  • Leaving out one of the parts when adding up the total.

Worked explanation

  1. From Figure 2: Canada 5, UK 7, Ireland 2, Australia 1 shops.
  2. Total shops = 5 + 7 + 2 + 1 = 15.
  3. UK share = 7 ÷ 15 × 100 = 46.666…%.
  4. To two decimal places: 46.67%.
Model answer · written by olevels.com, not an official Pearson mark scheme

Total shops = 5 + 7 + 2 + 1 = 15. Percentage in the UK = 7 ÷ 15 × 100 = 46.67%.

How the marks are earned

Mark 1: adding the total of 15 (or setting up 7 ÷ 15 × 100). Mark 2: 46.67%. The correct answer alone earns both.

Calculation check

  • 5+7+2+1 = 15
  • 7 ÷ (5+7+2+1) × 100 = 46.67
