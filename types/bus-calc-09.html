Knowledge card

One part as a percentage of a total

Calculation

Percentage share = part ÷ total × 100. The total is the whole market or all the parts added up. Market share is one firm's sales out of everyone's.

The method

  1. Find the part

    The firm or place asked for.

  2. Find the total

    Add the parts if needed.

  3. Divide, × 100

    Part ÷ total × 100.

  4. Round, add %

    As the question asks.

Do not lose marks here

  • ✗
    Wrong part

    Using another firm's figure.

  • ✗
    Upside down

    Total ÷ part.

  • ✗
    Missing a part

    Leaving one out when adding.

Worked explanation

  1. From Figure 2: Canada 5, UK 7, Ireland 2, Australia 1 shops.
  2. Total shops = 5 + 7 + 2 + 1 = 15.
  3. UK share = 7 ÷ 15 × 100 = 46.666…%.
  4. To two decimal places: 46.67%.
Model answer · written by olevels.com, not an official Pearson mark scheme

Total shops = 5 + 7 + 2 + 1 = 15. Percentage in the UK = 7 ÷ 15 × 100 = 46.67%.

How the marks are earned

Mark 1: adding the total of 15 (or setting up 7 ÷ 15 × 100). Mark 2: 46.67%. The correct answer alone earns both.

Calculation check

  • 5+7+2+1 = 15
  • 7 ÷ (5+7+2+1) × 100 = 46.67
