Market research data: types and reliability

Written

Quantitative data is numbers; qualitative data is opinions and reasons. Social media gathers data quickly and cheaply, but data must be reliable or decisions go wrong.

(g) Analyse the benefits to Kraft Heinz of using social media to collect market research data. (6)

Written-answer check
After your attempt, see the full worked explanation

Worked explanation

  1. Command: Analyse, 6 marks, levels-based: aim for two points, each applied to Kraft Heinz and developed.
  2. Pull case facts: global food business, new tomato sauce variety, changed Mac&Cheese recipe.
  3. Point 1: social media reaches huge numbers of people worldwide, so feedback on the new sauce comes in fast.
  4. Point 2: customers' comments on the new recipe give reasons (qualitative data) that guide changes.
  5. Finish each point with the decision Kraft Heinz can make: keep, adjust or stop the product.
Model answer · written by olevels.com, not an official Pearson mark scheme

Kraft Heinz sells its food all over the world, and social media is used by very large numbers of people in many countries. By posting a poll or asking followers to comment on its new variety of tomato sauce, Kraft Heinz can collect data from customers in many markets within days, and at a much lower cost than running a separate survey in each country. This means it quickly learns whether the new sauce is popular, so it can decide early whether to produce more of it, change it or withdraw it before large losses build up.

Social media also lets customers explain in their own words what they think of the changed Mac&Cheese recipe. These comments are qualitative data, showing why people like or dislike the new taste, which numbers alone would not show. Kraft Heinz can use these reasons to adjust the recipe so that it suits customers better, which protects sales of a well-known product and the reputation of the brand.

How the marks are earned

AO2 (3): applies the benefits to Kraft Heinz's global reach, the new tomato sauce and the Mac&Cheese recipe. AO3 (3): each point is followed by a logical chain from fast, low-cost or detailed data to a product decision and its effect on sales or reputation. Detailed and consistent, so Level 3 (5-6).
