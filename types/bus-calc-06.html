Break-even output using contribution

Finding break-even level of output as fixed costs divided by contribution per unit (price minus variable cost per unit).

Type 8 Calculations BUS-CALC-06

★★★☆☆ · 8 of 32 eligible examination papers, 2019–2025. Difficulty: Medium.

Asks how many units, people or items must be sold to break even, and gives fixed costs, variable cost per unit and selling price.

Break-even output using contribution

Calculation

Break-even output = fixed costs ÷ contribution per unit. Contribution = selling price − variable cost per unit. The answer is in units, rounded up.

The calculation

Break-even output = fixed costs ÷ contribution per unit

Contribution per unit = selling price − variable cost per unit.

Tap a part to see what it means and how to find it.

To find Contribution per unit:P − VC

To find Break-even revenue:break-even output × P

Try itFixed costs $9 000, price $30, variable cost $12 a unit. Break-even output?

  1. Contribution = $30 − $12 = $18
  2. Break-even = $9 000 ÷ $18 = 500 units

Exam question

A gym sells day passes at $15. Variable cost per pass is $5 and fixed costs are $4 000 a month. Calculate the break-even output. (3 marks)

Decide first: would this answer earn the mark? Then tap it to check.

Tap an answer to see if it earns the mark.

5

The method

  1. Find three figures

    Fixed costs, price, variable cost.

  2. Contribution

    Price − variable cost.

  3. Divide

    Fixed costs ÷ contribution.

  4. Round up, label

    Whole units, such as 400 passes.

Do not lose marks here

  • ✗
    Dividing by price

    Use contribution instead.

  • ✗
    Dividing by VC

    Variable cost alone is wrong.

  • ✗
    Money answer

    Break-even output is in units.

  • ✗
    Rounding down

    Leaves a small loss.

Your answer

Final-value check

After your attempt, see the full worked explanation

Worked explanation

  1. From Figure 4: monthly fixed costs €1 000, variable cost per unit €3.25, selling price €4.50.
  2. Contribution per unit = 4.50 − 3.25 = €1.25.
  3. Break-even output = 1 000 ÷ 1.25 = 800.
  4. 800 is a whole number, so no rounding is needed. Answer in units: 800 dog treats a month.
Model answer · written by olevels.com, not an official Pearson mark scheme

Contribution per unit = €4.50 − €3.25 = €1.25. Break-even output = fixed costs ÷ contribution per unit = €1 000 ÷ €1.25 = 800 dog treats.

How the marks are earned

Mark 1: the correct set-up, 1 000 ÷ (4.50 − 3.25). Mark 2: 800 dog treats. Writing 800 with no working earns both marks.

Calculation check

  • 4.50-3.25 = 1.25
  • 1000 ÷ (4.50-3.25) = 800
