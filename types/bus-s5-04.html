Knowledge card

Capital-intensive and labour-intensive production

Written

Capital-intensive production uses more machinery than labour. Labour-intensive production uses more workers than machinery. It is about who does most of the work, not about money.

The method

  1. Ask who works

    Machines or people do most of it?

  2. Compare

    Write more machinery than labour, or the reverse.

  3. Rule out

    Lean methods and types are not intensity.

Do not lose marks here

  • ✗
    Money

    It is not about cost of funds.

  • ✗
    No comparison

    Always compare machines with labour.

  • ✗
    Swapped

    Capital is machinery.

Worked explanation

  1. Command is Define for 1 mark: one precise sentence.
  2. Identify the input relied on: labour (workers).
  3. Add the comparison with machinery so the meaning is complete.
Model answer · written by olevels.com, not an official Pearson mark scheme

Labour-intensive production is a way of producing that relies more on workers than on machinery and equipment.

How the marks are earned

1 mark for stating that the method uses more labour (people) than capital (machines). The comparison is what earns the mark.
