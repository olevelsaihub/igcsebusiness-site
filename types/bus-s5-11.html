Technology and robotics in production

Written

Robotics uses programmed machines to do tasks. They raise productivity and consistency, but cost a lot, cut jobs and are less flexible than people.

4

The method

  1. Name a gain

    Fewer errors, faster, 24/7 or less labour.

  2. Say how

    Programmed to repeat, no breaks.

  3. End with result

    Quality, productivity, unit cost or profit.

  4. Weigh up

    Set the gain against cost and job losses.

Do not lose marks here

  • ✗
    Saying "better"

    Say how and with what result.

  • ✗
    Cheap robots

    They cost a lot to buy.

  • ✗
    Generic

    Use the named business.

After your attempt, see the full worked explanation

Worked explanation

  1. Command is Explain for 3 marks: one reason plus two linked developments.
  2. Reason: robots reduce production errors.
  3. First link: why? They are programmed to repeat the same task identically and do not tire.
  4. Second link: so products are made to a consistent standard and fewer faulty items are produced.
  5. Stick to one reason; do not also list speed or 24/7 working.
Model answer · written by olevels.com, not an official Pearson mark scheme

Using robots reduces mistakes in production. A robot can be programmed to carry out exactly the same movement in exactly the same way every time, and it does not get tired or bored like a person might. This means every product is made to the same standard, so quality is more consistent and fewer faulty items have to be thrown away or repaired.

How the marks are earned

1 mark for the reason (fewer production errors). 1 mark for how (programmed to repeat identically without tiring). 1 mark for the result (consistent, higher quality and fewer faulty items). Total 3.
