Functional areas and the human resources role

What human resources, finance, marketing and production departments do, and why businesses have a human resources function.

Type 3 2 People in business BUS-S2-34

★★★★★ · 20 of 32 eligible examination papers, 2019–2025. Difficulty: Easy.

The question asks which department or functional area does a task, or names human resources, finance, marketing or production and asks for a task or responsibility.

KnowledgeMethodWorked examplePracticeTeach it back

Knowledge

  • A functional area (department) is a part of a business that carries out one type of work.
  • Human resources (HR) deals with people: workforce planning, recruitment and selection, training, health and safety, staff welfare, discipline, grievances and dismissal.
  • Finance deals with money: paying wages and salaries, paying suppliers, sending invoices, budgets, cash-flow forecasts and accounts.
  • Marketing deals with customers: market research, pricing, advertising, sales promotion, public relations, customer service and distribution.
  • Production makes the product: manufacturing, designing new products, quality control and stock control.
  • Having an HR function means the business has the right number of suitable, trained staff and follows employment law.

Before you begin

  • What a department is
  • Recruitment, training and health and safety
  • The difference between costs, customers and products

Specification: 2.2.3 · 2.5.3

Answer technique: Explain: point plus two linked expansions (3 marks) · Answer a multiple-choice question · State or give a point from the case (1 mark)

Method

  1. Find the task in the question and match it to its department. Why: Most multiple-choice items test this matching.
  2. For State questions, describe a task using the business's own products or staff. Why: Tasks must be in context to earn the mark.
  3. For Explain questions, name one responsibility (such as recruitment). Why: This earns the identification mark.
  4. Describe what the department actually does for it, then the result for the business. Why: The two linked steps earn the development marks.

How each command word is answered

Command wordTimes askedWhat to write
State7One task that fits the department and the business, for example finance paying L.table staff or production testing Saha's robots.
multiple-choice10Match the task to its home: money to finance, people to HR, customers and promotion to marketing, making and quality control to production.
Explain4One HR responsibility, then what HR does for it, then the result such as the right person hired or staff kept safe.

Common mistakes

Inferred, not measured: Inferred from the official schemes' credit and rejection patterns; not measured student-error statistics.

  • Putting paying wages under HR rather than finance (HR plans staffing; finance pays the money).
  • Putting quality control under marketing instead of production.
  • In State questions, naming a task that ignores the named business and its products.
  • Listing three HR responsibilities with no explanation.

Worked explanation

  1. Explain card: one responsibility plus two linked expansions.
  2. Responsibility: recruitment and selection of employees.
  3. Expansion 1: what HR does: writes job documents, advertises, shortlists and interviews.
  4. Expansion 2: the result: the best-matched person is appointed, works well and is less likely to leave.
Model answer · written by olevels.com, not an official Pearson mark scheme

One responsibility of human resources is recruiting and selecting new employees. The HR department writes the job description and person specification, advertises the vacancy, shortlists applicants and interviews them. This helps the business appoint the applicant whose skills best match the job, so the new employee can work effectively and is less likely to leave.

How the marks are earned

1 mark for the responsibility (recruitment and selection). 1 mark for describing what HR does in that process. 1 mark for the outcome: the most suitable person appointed, working effectively.
