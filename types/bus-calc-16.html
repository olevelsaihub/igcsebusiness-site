Return on capital employed (ROCE)

Calculation

ROCE % = operating profit ÷ capital employed × 100. It shows the operating profit made for every $ invested. Higher is better.

The method

  1. Find operating profit

    Gross profit − operating expenses if needed.

  2. Find capital employed

    The bottom of the fraction.

  3. Divide and × 100

    Operating profit ÷ capital employed × 100.

  4. Round and label

    Add % and the rounding asked.

Do not lose marks here

  • ✗
    Gross profit on top

    ROCE uses operating profit.

  • ✗
    Wrong line underneath

    Do not use current assets.

  • ✗
    Upside down

    Profit on top, capital employed below.

Worked explanation

  1. From Figure 2: operating profit €84 054, capital employed €266 058. Current assets and liabilities are not needed.
  2. ROCE = 84 054 ÷ 266 058 × 100 = 31.592…%.
  3. To two decimal places: 31.59%.
