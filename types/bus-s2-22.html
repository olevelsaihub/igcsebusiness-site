Why motivating and keeping staff matters

Written

Motivated staff work harder, make fewer mistakes and are less likely to leave. That means better service, lower recruitment costs and more sales and profit.

Explain one reason why a business might pay above the minimum wage. (3 marks)

4

The method

  1. Give a reason

    Pick one: productivity, service, lower turnover or attracting skilled staff.

  2. Explain the link

    Show how motivation or retention causes that effect.

  3. Reach the business

    Carry it to costs, sales or profit.

  4. Use the case

    Name the jobs and products in the question.

Do not lose marks here

  • ✗
    How, not why

    Do not describe bonuses when the question asks why motivation matters.

  • ✗
    Stopping at "work harder"

    Add what that does to sales, costs or profit.

  • ✗
    A list of benefits

    One developed reason beats three bare ones.

  • ✗
    Not in context

    Refer to the named business and its staff.

After your attempt, see the full worked explanation

Worked explanation

  1. Explain card: one benefit plus two linked expansions.
  2. Benefit: motivated staff are more likely to stay, so staff turnover is lower.
  3. Expansion 1: the business avoids repeated advertising, interviewing and training.
  4. Expansion 2: money saved can be used elsewhere, and experienced staff give better service.
Model answer · written by olevels.com, not an official Pearson mark scheme

Motivated employees are more likely to stay with the business, so its staff turnover is lower. This means the business does not have to keep advertising vacancies, interviewing applicants and training replacements. As a result it saves time and money that can be used elsewhere, and experienced staff who stay can give customers a better service, helping to increase sales.

How the marks are earned

1 mark for the benefit (staff stay, lower turnover). 1 mark for explaining the recruitment and training avoided. 1 mark for the consequence: cost and time saved, used elsewhere, with better service.
