Motivation theories: Maslow, Taylor and Herzberg

Written

Taylor said money motivates. Maslow said needs run in five levels, and lower needs come first. Herzberg said hygiene factors only stop unhappiness, while motivators really motivate.

Safety (level 2)

Why
Job security is a safety need.
Order
Basic, safety, social, esteem, self-actualisation.

Social (level 3)

Why
Belonging to a team is a social need.

Esteem (level 4)

Why
Recognition and status are esteem needs.
Idea
Lower needs must be met before higher ones motivate.

4

The method

  1. Name the theory

    Is it Taylor, Maslow or Herzberg? Each has its own idea.

  2. Place the factor

    Find its level or its type, hygiene or motivator.

  3. Give an example

    Use something from the workplace, such as a pay rise or an award.

  4. Link to effort

    Say how it changes satisfaction or the effort workers give.

Do not lose marks here

  • ✗
    Mixing up theorists

    Hygiene factors belong to Herzberg, not Maslow.

  • ✗
    Counting from the top

    Maslow's basic needs are level 1, at the bottom.

  • ✗
    Pay as a motivator

    For Herzberg, pay only prevents dissatisfaction.

  • ✗
    Naming with no reason

    Always explain how the factor changes workers' behaviour.

Worked explanation

  1. Explain card: one factor plus two linked expansions; do not try to explain both factors.
  2. Pick a motivator factor.
  3. Expansion 1: what it is, with an example such as recognition or responsibility giving a sense of achievement.
  4. Expansion 2: the effect: staff feel satisfied, so they work harder and are more productive.
Model answer · written by olevels.com, not an official Pearson mark scheme

One factor in Herzberg's theory is a motivator, such as being given more responsibility or being recognised for good work. Herzberg said motivators come from the job itself, so an employee gains a feeling of achievement and personal growth when they do the work well. Because of this, employees become more satisfied and genuinely motivated, so they put in more effort and are more productive.

How the marks are earned

1 mark for naming the factor (a motivator). 1 mark for explaining it with an example (achievement, recognition, responsibility from the job itself). 1 mark for the effect on the worker (satisfaction, effort, productivity).
