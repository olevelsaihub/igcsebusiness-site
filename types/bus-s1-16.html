Knowledge card

Multinationals and globalisation

Written

A multinational operates in more than one country. Globalisation is the world-wide growth in trade and business links. Always check whether a question asks about the business or the host country.

Explain one benefit to a country of a multinational locating there. (3 marks)

4

The method

  1. Whose side?

    The business itself or the host country.

  2. Pick one gain

    Bigger market, lower costs, spread risk, or new jobs.

  3. Follow it on

    Firm: sales, costs, profit. Country: jobs, taxes, living standards.

  4. Use the case

    For Analyse, use two benefits applied to the named business.

Do not lose marks here

  • ✗
    Wrong side

    Business gains and country gains are different answers.

  • ✗
    Definition

    Do not define the term when asked for a benefit.

  • ✗
    Mixed terms

    Globalisation is a trend; a multinational is one business.

  • ✗
    No link

    A benefit needs a result: revenue, costs or jobs.

Written-answer check
After your attempt, see the full worked explanation

Worked explanation

  1. Read the command: Explain one benefit, 3 marks; no definition needed.
  2. Pick one benefit: it sells in many countries, so its risk is spread.
  3. Link it: a fall in sales in one country is balanced by sales elsewhere.
  4. Add the consequence: total revenue is steadier, so the business is safer.
Model answer · written by olevels.com, not an official Pearson mark scheme

One benefit of being a multinational is that the business spreads its risk across many countries. For example, Huawei sells smartphones and tablets in many different markets, so if demand falls in one country, perhaps because of a recession there, strong sales in other countries can make up for it. This keeps its total revenue more stable, so the business is less likely to face cash problems or have to cut production.

How the marks are earned

1 mark for the benefit (risk spread across countries). 1 mark for the link (falling sales in one country balanced by sales elsewhere). 1 mark for the consequence (steadier revenue, a safer business). Total 3.
