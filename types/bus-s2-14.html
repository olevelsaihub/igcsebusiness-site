Equal opportunities and minimum wage laws

Written

Equal opportunities laws stop unfair treatment because of gender, race, disability, religion, sexual orientation or age. The minimum wage is the lowest hourly pay allowed by law.

The method

  1. Name the law

    Equal opportunities or minimum wage?

  2. Say what the firm must do

    Pay equally or adapt the workplace.

  3. Give the reason

    Avoid legal action, treat staff fairly, widen the talent pool.

  4. End with a result

    Reputation, costs or better motivation.

Do not lose marks here

  • ✗
    Skills are not protected

    Choosing on ability is allowed.

  • ✗
    Minimum wage

    It is hourly pay, not a bonus or overtime.

  • ✗
    Listing groups

    Explain a consequence, not just names.

Written-answer check
After your attempt, see the full worked explanation

Worked explanation

  1. Explain card: one reason plus two linked expansions; no definition needed.
  2. Reason: it is the law, and breaking it can lead to legal action.
  3. Expansion 1: unfair treatment because of age, gender or race lets the person take the business to a tribunal or court.
  4. Expansion 2: compensation, legal costs and bad publicity hurt the business's finances and reputation.
Model answer · written by olevels.com, not an official Pearson mark scheme

Businesses comply because equal opportunities is the law, and breaking it can lead to legal action. If a business treats an applicant or employee unfairly because of their age, gender, race or disability, that person can take the business to an employment tribunal or court. The business might then have to pay compensation and legal costs, and the bad publicity could damage its reputation, making it harder to attract customers and good employees.

How the marks are earned

1 mark for the reason (legal requirement, risk of legal action). 1 mark for explaining unfair treatment because of a protected characteristic. 1 mark for the consequence: compensation, costs and reputation damage. A definition alone earns nothing.
