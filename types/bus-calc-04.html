Total revenue or total pay: rate times quantity

Multiplying a unit price or pay rate by a quantity (items, months, hours, employees) to find total revenue or total remuneration.

Knowledge card

Total revenue or total pay: rate times quantity

Calculation

Total = rate × quantity. Use the selling price for revenue and the pay rate for wages. Add groups first, and count the whole time period.

The calculation

Total = rate × quantity

Revenue = price × units sold. Pay = rate × hours × people.

Tap a part to see what it means and how to find it.

To find Revenue:price × units sold

To find Total pay:rate × hours × staff

Try itA café sells 120 cups a day at $3.50. Revenue over 30 days?

  1. Cups = 120 × 30 = 3 600
  2. Revenue = 3 600 × $3.50 = $12 600

5

The method

  1. Find the rate

    Price per item or pay per hour.

  2. Find the quantity

    Add groups. Count every month or week.

  3. Multiply

    Rate × total quantity.

  4. Label it

    Currency and rounding asked.

Do not lose marks here

  • ✗
    Short period

    Working out one month, not the year.

  • ✗
    One group only

    Missing a factory or shop.

  • ✗
    Wrong figure

    Using cost, not price.

(e) Calculate the total revenue from the sale of all battery packs sold by Saha in 2023. You are advised to show your working. (2)

After your attempt, see the full worked explanation

Worked explanation

  1. Price per battery pack = $90. Sales = 5 packs every month in 2023, so 12 months.
  2. Revenue per month = 90 × 5 = $450.
  3. Revenue for the year = 450 × 12 = $5 400.
Model answer · written by olevels.com, not an official Pearson mark scheme

Total revenue = price × quantity = $90 × (5 × 12) = $90 × 60 = $5 400.

How the marks are earned

Mark 1: a correct set-up such as 90 × 5 or 5 × 12. Mark 2: the full year total of $5 400. The correct total by itself earns both.

Calculation check

  • 90 × 5 = 450
  • 5 × 12 = 60
  • 90 × 5 × 12 = 5400
