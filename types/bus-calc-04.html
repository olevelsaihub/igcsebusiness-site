Total revenue or total pay: rate times quantity

Multiplying a unit price or pay rate by a quantity (items, months, hours, employees) to find total revenue or total remuneration.

Type 7 Calculations BUS-CALC-04

★☆☆☆☆ · 4 of 32 eligible examination papers, 2019–2025. Difficulty: Easy.

Asks for total revenue, total pay or total remuneration, and gives a price or pay rate plus a number of items, hours, months or employees.

Knowledge

  • Revenue is the money a business receives from selling its products: revenue = price per unit × quantity sold.
  • Remuneration is the total reward paid to employees. Total pay = pay rate × hours worked, or pay per employee × number of employees.
  • Check the time period. 'Five every month' for a year means 5 × 12 months.
  • If there are several groups (factories, shops), add the numbers in each group first, then multiply by the rate.
  • Use the selling price for revenue, not the variable cost or the fixed cost.
  • Give money to 2 decimal places when asked and write the currency.

Before you begin

  • What revenue means
  • Multiplying by 12 for a year of months
  • Adding several numbers before multiplying

Specification: 3.3.1

Answer technique: Set out a calculation with working (2 marks) · Answer a multiple-choice question

Method

  1. Find the rate (price per item, pay per hour or per day). Why: This is the number you multiply by.
  2. Find every quantity, including the time period, and add groups together if needed. Why: Missing a month or a factory gives a wrong total.
  3. Multiply rate × total quantity. Why: Revenue and pay both follow rate × quantity.
  4. Write the answer with the currency and the rounding asked for. Why: Shows the answer is complete.

Common mistakes

Inferred, not measured: Inferred from the official schemes' credit and rejection patterns; not measured student-error statistics.

  • Forgetting the time period, for example working out one month instead of the whole year.
  • Multiplying by only one group (one factory) instead of the total of all groups.
  • Using the fixed cost or the variable cost in place of the price.

Worked explanation

  1. Price per battery pack = $90. Sales = 5 packs every month in 2023, so 12 months.
  2. Revenue per month = 90 × 5 = $450.
  3. Revenue for the year = 450 × 12 = $5 400.
Model answer · written by olevels.com, not an official Pearson mark scheme

Total revenue = price × quantity = $90 × (5 × 12) = $90 × 60 = $5 400.

How the marks are earned

Mark 1: a correct set-up such as 90 × 5 or 5 × 12. Mark 2: the full year total of $5 400. The correct total by itself earns both.

Calculation check

  • 90 × 5 = 450
  • 5 × 12 = 60
  • 90 × 5 × 12 = 5400
