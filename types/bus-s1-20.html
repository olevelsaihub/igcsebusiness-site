Knowledge card

Government spending, taxation and laws

Written

Governments tax to raise money and spend it on public services such as schools, hospitals and police. Laws make businesses change what they do, which can raise costs but protect people.

The method

  1. Name the action

    Spending, tax or a law.

  2. What changes

    Say what it forces or allows.

  3. Follow on

    Take it to costs, prices, demand or health.

  4. Use the case

    Apply it to the named business.

Do not lose marks here

  • ✗
    Taxes and demand

    Governments tax to raise money, not to raise demand.

  • ✗
    Private shop

    A shop is not a public service.

  • ✗
    Law only

    Explain why the law was needed and what it changes.

  • ✗
    General effect

    Make the effect fit the named business.

After your attempt, see the full worked explanation

Worked explanation

  1. Read the command: Analyse, 6 marks; two connected, applied points.
  2. Point 1: less sugar improves customers' health.
  3. Point 2: healthier people mean less pressure and cost on the health service.
  4. Link to the case: chocolate makers like Cadbury must change their recipes.
Model answer · written by olevels.com, not an official Pearson mark scheme

Governments have passed laws to cut the sugar in chocolate mainly to improve people's health. Eating too much sugar can cause people to become overweight and can lead to illnesses such as diabetes and tooth decay. If makers such as Cadbury must reduce the sugar in bars like Dairy Milk, customers will take in less sugar each time they eat chocolate, even if they do not change their habits.

A second reason is to reduce the cost to the country of treating these illnesses. When fewer people suffer from sugar-related illnesses, fewer need treatment from the health service. This lowers the demand on doctors and hospitals and reduces government spending on health care, so the money saved can be used for other public services such as schools.

A law also applies to every chocolate maker, so no single firm loses out to rivals by making its products healthier.

How the marks are earned

Level 3 (5-6): reasons applied to sugar in chocolate and Cadbury's brands (AO2, 3 marks), with logical chains from less sugar to better health and from fewer illnesses to lower health spending (AO3, 3 marks). Total 6.
