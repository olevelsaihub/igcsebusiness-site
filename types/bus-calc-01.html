Convert prices between currencies with exchange rates

Converting an amount from one currency to another using a given exchange rate, including totalling quantity times unit price before or after conversion.

Type 15 Calculations BUS-CALC-01

★★★★★ · 21 of 32 eligible examination papers, 2019–2025. Difficulty: Medium.

The question gives an exchange rate line such as '£1 = 9.35 CNY' and asks how much is paid, or what the price is, in a different currency.

Convert prices between currencies with exchange rates

Calculation

Find the currency with the 1 in front of it. Going from that currency to the other, multiply by the rate. Going back, divide. Do any quantity × price first.

Working

Why

Working

Zloty to euro: divide

Why
The 1 is next to €, so PLN to € means ÷ 4.50.
Working
1 800 ÷ 4.50 = €400

Does the size fit?

Why
One euro is worth many zloty, so the € number must be smaller.

Which side has the 1?

Rule
The 1 is on the euro side, so euros are the 'base' currency.

2

The calculation

New amount = amount × rate (or ÷ rate)

From the currency with the 1: multiply. Towards it: divide.

Tap a part to see what it means and how to find it.

To find Total before converting:Q × P

To find Amount in other currency:total × R

To find Amount in the 1 currency:total ÷ R

Try itA café in Lima buys 25 kg of tea at £8 a kg. £1 = 4.60 PEN. Total cost in PEN?

  1. Total = 25 × £8 = £200
  2. In PEN = £200 × 4.60 = 920 PEN

5

The method

  1. Mark the rate

    Write 1 [currency] = x and underline the currency you need.

  2. Total first

    Quantity × price in the starting currency.

  3. Multiply or divide

    From the 1 currency: × rate. Back to it: ÷ rate.

  4. Round and label

    Usually 2 decimal places, with the code.

  5. Sense check

    Is the number bigger or smaller, as it should be?

Do not lose marks here

  • ✗
    Wrong direction

    Multiplying when you should divide.

  • ✗
    Missing quantity

    Converting one item, not all of them.

  • ✗
    Adding the rate

    The rate is multiplied or divided, never added.

  • ✗
    Wrong currency

    Leaving the answer in the starting currency.

Worked explanation

  1. The rate is €1 = 5 MYR, so the euro is the '1' currency.
  2. The bill is in euros (€2 250) and the answer must be in MYR, so we go from the '1' currency to the other: multiply.
  3. 2 250 × 5 = 11 250 MYR.
  4. Sense check: each euro is worth 5 ringgits, so the MYR figure should be bigger. 11 250 is bigger. Match to option C.
Model answer · written by olevels.com, not an official Pearson mark scheme

Cost in MYR = €2 250 × 5 = 11 250 MYR. Answer: C (11 250).

How the marks are earned

One mark for choosing option C. The 150 kg is extra information; the €2 250 is already the total cost, so it is converted directly.

Calculation check

  • 2250 × 5 = 11250
