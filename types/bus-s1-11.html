Knowledge card

Primary, secondary and tertiary sectors

Written

Ask what the business actually does. Primary takes raw materials from the earth or sea, secondary makes goods, tertiary provides services. A shop selling goods is tertiary.

4

The method

  1. What does it do?

    Extract, make, or provide a service.

  2. Match

    Extract is primary, make is secondary, service is tertiary.

  3. Give the reason

    Say the activity, not just the name of the sector.

  4. Add case detail

    For an Outline, show the activity in the business.

Do not lose marks here

  • ✗
    Shops are not secondary

    Retail sells goods but is a service.

  • ✗
    Example only

    Name the activity, not just an example.

  • ✗
    No case detail

    An Outline needs a detail from the case.

(b) Outline why AWG can be classified as operating in the secondary sector. (2)

Hint for part (b)

Written-answer check
After your attempt, see the full worked explanation

Worked explanation

  1. Read the command: Outline, 2 marks, about AWG specifically.
  2. State the rule: secondary means making goods from raw materials.
  3. Apply it with case facts: wool turned into jumpers and dresses in AWG's workshops.
Model answer · written by olevels.com, not an official Pearson mark scheme

AWG is in the secondary sector because it manufactures goods. In its own workshops it takes natural raw materials such as wool and makes them into finished clothing such as jumpers, dresses and baby clothes.

How the marks are earned

1 mark for the reason (it makes goods from raw materials). 1 mark for applying it to AWG (wool made into jumpers, dresses and baby clothes in its workshops). Total 2.
