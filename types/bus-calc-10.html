Find a percentage of an amount

Finding X% of a value as the answer itself: commission, bonus, interest, a discount amount, or a share of a workforce.

Type 11 Calculations BUS-CALC-10

★★☆☆☆ · 6 of 32 eligible examination papers, 2019–2025. Difficulty: Medium.

Asks for the commission, bonus, interest, discount or number of people that a given percentage represents.

Knowledge

  • To find x% of an amount: amount × x ÷ 100 (for example 5% of £850 = 850 × 0.05).
  • Commission is pay based on a percentage of the value of sales made.
  • A bonus is an extra payment on top of normal pay, often a percentage of earnings.
  • Interest is the charge for borrowing money, usually a percentage of the loan or overdraft.
  • In this type the answer is the percentage amount itself (the commission, the interest, the discount), NOT the new total.
  • If several items or people are involved, find the full base first (price × number). Money answers normally go to 2 decimal places.

Before you begin

  • Converting a percentage to a decimal
  • Meaning of commission, bonus and interest
  • Rounding money to 2 decimal places

Answer technique: Set out a calculation with working (2 marks) · Answer a multiple-choice question

Method

  1. Find the base amount and multiply out any quantities first (price × units). Why: The percentage must be taken of the whole base.
  2. Change the percentage to a decimal: divide by 100. Why: 5% becomes 0.05 for the calculator.
  3. Multiply base × decimal. Why: This gives the percentage amount.
  4. Stop there; round as asked and add the unit. Why: Adding it on would answer a different question.

Common mistakes

Inferred, not measured: Inferred from the official schemes' credit and rejection patterns; not measured student-error statistics.

  • Adding the percentage on and giving the new total instead of the amount.
  • Using the wrong percentage, or subtracting the percentage number itself (e.g. 1 400 − 12).
  • Forgetting to multiply by the number of items or people.
  • Putting the decimal point in the wrong place (using 0.5 for 5%).

Worked explanation

  1. Commission rate = 5%. Value of goods sold = £850.
  2. 5% as a decimal = 0.05.
  3. Commission = 850 × 0.05 = 42.5.
  4. To two decimal places: £42.50.
Model answer · written by olevels.com, not an official Pearson mark scheme

Commission = 5% of £850 = 850 × 0.05 = £42.50.

How the marks are earned

Mark 1: setting up 850 × 0.05 (or 5%). Mark 2: £42.50. The correct answer alone gets both.

Calculation check

  • 850 × 0.05 = 42.5
  • 850 × 5 ÷ 100 = 42.5
