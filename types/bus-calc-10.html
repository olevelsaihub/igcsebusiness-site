Find a percentage of an amount

Calculation

x% of an amount = amount × x ÷ 100. Find the full base first. The answer is the commission, bonus, interest or discount itself, not the new total.

The calculation

x% of an amount = amount × x ÷ 100

Turn x% into a decimal: 5% = 0.05, 12% = 0.12.

Tap a part to see what it means and how to find it.

To find Decimal:x ÷ 100

To find Answer:A × x ÷ 100

Try itA worker gets a 5% bonus on earnings of $2 840. Bonus?

  1. 5 ÷ 100 = 0.05
  2. $2 840 × 0.05 = $142

The method

  1. Find the base

    Multiply out quantities first.

  2. To a decimal

    Divide the % by 100.

  3. Multiply

    Base × decimal.

  4. Stop there

    Round and add the unit.

Do not lose marks here

  • ✗
    Adding on

    Giving the new total.

  • ✗
    Taking away the number

    Doing 1 400 − 12.

  • ✗
    One item only

    Forgetting the quantity.

  • ✗
    Decimal slip

    Using 0.5 for 5%.

(a) Calculate, to two decimal places, the commission a sales assistant would earn if they have sold goods valued at £850. You are advised to show your working. (2)  

After your attempt, see the full worked explanation

Worked explanation

  1. Commission rate = 5%. Value of goods sold = £850.
  2. 5% as a decimal = 0.05.
  3. Commission = 850 × 0.05 = 42.5.
  4. To two decimal places: £42.50.
Model answer · written by olevels.com, not an official Pearson mark scheme

Commission = 5% of £850 = 850 × 0.05 = £42.50.

How the marks are earned

Mark 1: setting up 850 × 0.05 (or 5%). Mark 2: £42.50. The correct answer alone gets both.

Calculation check

  • 850 × 0.05 = 42.5
  • 850 × 5 ÷ 100 = 42.5
