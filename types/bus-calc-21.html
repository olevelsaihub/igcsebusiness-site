Labour productivity: output per hour

Calculation

Labour productivity = output ÷ labour input (hours or workers). Always state the unit, such as phones per hour.

The method

  1. Find the output

    The top of the division.

  2. Find the labour input

    Hours or workers.

  3. Divide, add the unit

    Output ÷ input, such as units per hour.

Do not lose marks here

  • ✗
    Upside down

    Output goes on top.

  • ✗
    No unit

    Say per hour or per worker.

  • ✗
    Multiplying

    Productivity is a division.

Final-value check
smartphones per hour
After your attempt, see the full worked explanation

Worked explanation

  1. Output = 1 000 smartphones. Time = 20 hours.
  2. Productivity = 1 000 ÷ 20 = 50.
  3. Answer with unit: 50 smartphones per hour.
Model answer · written by olevels.com, not an official Pearson mark scheme

Labour productivity = output ÷ hours = 1 000 ÷ 20 = 50 smartphones per hour.

How the marks are earned

Mark 1: 1 000 ÷ 20 set up. Mark 2: 50. The correct answer alone earns both.
