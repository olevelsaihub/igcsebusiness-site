Work out profit or loss

Calculating profit or loss as revenue minus all costs, gross profit per unit times sales, or operating profit as gross profit minus expenses.

Type 13 Calculations BUS-CALC-05

★☆☆☆☆ · 5 of 32 eligible examination papers, 2019–2025. Difficulty: Medium.

Asks for the profit, gross profit, operating profit, or which statement (profit or loss of a figure) is correct, using revenue and cost figures.

KnowledgeMethodWorked examplePracticeTeach it back

Knowledge

  • Profit = total revenue − total costs. If the answer is negative, the business has made a loss.
  • Total costs means every cost line added together: labour, materials, other costs, or fixed + variable costs.
  • Gross profit = revenue − cost of sales (the direct cost of the goods sold). Per unit: (price − cost per unit) × units sold.
  • Operating profit = gross profit − operating expenses (overheads such as rent and salaries).
  • Write 'profit' or 'loss' clearly and give the currency. Large figures are usually whole numbers.

Before you begin

  • Meaning of revenue and costs
  • Difference between cost of sales and operating expenses
  • Working with negative numbers

Learn these types first: Total cost from fixed and variable costs · Total revenue or total pay: rate times quantity

Specification: 3.3.1

Answer technique: Set out a calculation with working (2 marks) · Answer a multiple-choice question

Method

  1. Decide which profit is asked for: profit, gross profit or operating profit. Why: Each uses different lines.
  2. Add up all the relevant costs (or find revenue and cost of sales for the units sold). Why: Leaving out one cost line is a common trap.
  3. Subtract: revenue − total costs, or gross profit − operating expenses. Why: Profit is what is left after costs.
  4. Read the sign: positive is a profit, negative is a loss. State it with the currency. Why: Multiple-choice options test profit versus loss.

How each command word is answered

Command wordTimes askedWhat to write
multiple-choice4Options often give the total costs as if it were a profit, or flip profit and loss. Work out the full figure and its sign first. Use the Multiple-choice card.
Calculate1Use the Calculate card: show revenue and cost totals (or profit per unit × units), the subtraction, then the profit with currency.

Common mistakes

Inferred, not measured: Inferred from the official schemes' credit and rejection patterns; not measured student-error statistics.

  • Leaving out one of the cost lines, for example subtracting only labour costs.
  • Giving the total costs as the answer instead of the profit.
  • Subtracting the wrong way round and calling a profit a loss.
  • Subtracting operating expenses from sales instead of from gross profit, or treating gross profit as operating profit.

Work out profit or loss · June 2019 · Paper 2 · Regional (R) · Question 4 · part (a) · 2 marks

(a) Calculate the gross profit for Yellow if it sold 14,000 T-shirts. You are advised to show your working. (2)

View full original question and diagrams
Hint for part (a)

Decide which profit is asked for: overall, gross or operating. Gather every cost line that belongs in that level, take it from the money coming in, and label the result profit or loss.

Your answer

Work it out on paper, then type your final answer. Give it to 0 decimal places.

Final-value check
BDT
After your attempt, see the full worked explanation

Worked explanation

  1. Cost to make one T-shirt = 11.05 BDT; selling price = 13.75 BDT; T-shirts sold = 14 000.
  2. Revenue = 13.75 × 14 000 = 192 500 BDT.
  3. Cost of sales = 11.05 × 14 000 = 154 700 BDT.
  4. Gross profit = 192 500 − 154 700 = 37 800 BDT.
  5. Quick check by the per-unit route: (13.75 − 11.05) × 14 000 = 2.70 × 14 000 = 37 800 BDT.
Model answer · written by olevels.com, not an official Pearson mark scheme

Revenue = 13.75 × 14 000 = 192 500 BDT. Cost of sales = 11.05 × 14 000 = 154 700 BDT. Gross profit = 192 500 − 154 700 = 37 800 BDT.

How the marks are earned

Mark 1: a correct set-up, either revenue (13.75 × 14 000) or gross profit per unit (13.75 − 11.05). Mark 2: the gross profit of 37 800 BDT. The correct figure without working scores both.

Calculation check

  • 13.75 × 14000 = 192500
  • 11.05 × 14000 = 154700
  • 13.75 × 14000-11.05 × 14000 = 37800
  • (13.75-11.05) × 14000 = 37800
