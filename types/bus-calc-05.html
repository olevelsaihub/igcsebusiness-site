Work out profit or loss

Calculation

Profit = revenue − total costs. Add every cost line first. A negative answer is a loss. Gross profit = revenue − cost of sales; operating profit = gross profit − expenses.

5

The method

  1. Which profit?

    Profit, gross profit or operating profit.

  2. Add the costs

    Every relevant cost line.

  3. Subtract

    Revenue − costs, or gross profit − expenses.

  4. Read the sign

    Positive is profit, negative is loss.

Do not lose marks here

  • ✗
    Missing a cost

    Subtracting only some lines.

  • ✗
    Wrong answer

    Giving total costs as the profit.

  • ✗
    Wrong way round

    Calling a profit a loss.

  • ✗
    Wrong start

    Taking expenses from revenue.

Work out profit or loss · June 2019 · Paper 2 · Regional (R) · Question 4 · part (a) · 2 marks

(a) Calculate the gross profit for Yellow if it sold 14,000 T-shirts. You are advised to show your working. (2)

View full original question and diagrams
Hint for part (a)

Decide which profit is asked for: overall, gross or operating. Gather every cost line that belongs in that level, take it from the money coming in, and label the result profit or loss.

Your answer

Work it out on paper, then type your final answer. Give it to 0 decimal places.

Final-value check
BDT
After your attempt, see the full worked explanation

Worked explanation

  1. Cost to make one T-shirt = 11.05 BDT; selling price = 13.75 BDT; T-shirts sold = 14 000.
  2. Revenue = 13.75 × 14 000 = 192 500 BDT.
  3. Cost of sales = 11.05 × 14 000 = 154 700 BDT.
  4. Gross profit = 192 500 − 154 700 = 37 800 BDT.
  5. Quick check by the per-unit route: (13.75 − 11.05) × 14 000 = 2.70 × 14 000 = 37 800 BDT.
Model answer · written by olevels.com, not an official Pearson mark scheme

Revenue = 13.75 × 14 000 = 192 500 BDT. Cost of sales = 11.05 × 14 000 = 154 700 BDT. Gross profit = 192 500 − 154 700 = 37 800 BDT.

How the marks are earned

Mark 1: a correct set-up, either revenue (13.75 × 14 000) or gross profit per unit (13.75 − 11.05). Mark 2: the gross profit of 37 800 BDT. The correct figure without working scores both.

Calculation check

  • 13.75 × 14000 = 192500
  • 11.05 × 14000 = 154700
  • 13.75 × 14000-11.05 × 14000 = 37800
  • (13.75-11.05) × 14000 = 37800
