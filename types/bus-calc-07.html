Knowledge card

Net cash flow and closing balance

Calculation

Net cash flow = cash in − cash out. Closing balance = opening balance + net cash flow. Each month's closing balance is the next month's opening balance.

5

The method

  1. Total each side

    Add all inflows, all outflows.

  2. Net

    Inflows − outflows. Keep the sign.

  3. Closing

    Opening balance + net.

  4. Missing line?

    Rearrange the same sums.

  5. Read the ask

    Net cash flow or closing balance?

Do not lose marks here

  • ✗
    Adding both

    Inflows and outflows are not added.

  • ✗
    No opening

    Forgetting the opening balance.

  • ✗
    Wrong sign

    Subtracting the wrong way round.

  • ✗
    Wrong figure

    Giving closing when net was asked.

Worked explanation

  1. From Figure 3: opening balance 16 000, cash inflow 22 000, cash outflow 20 000.
  2. Net cash flow = 22 000 − 20 000 = 2 000.
  3. Closing balance = 16 000 + 2 000 = 18 000.
Model answer · written by olevels.com, not an official Pearson mark scheme

Net cash flow = cash inflow − cash outflow = 22 000 − 20 000 = 2 000. Closing balance = opening balance + net cash flow = 16 000 + 2 000 = €18 000.

How the marks are earned

Mark 1: the net cash flow of 2 000 (or the correct set-up). Mark 2: the closing balance of 18 000. The correct closing balance alone gets both marks.

Calculation check

  • 22000-20000 = 2000
  • 16000+(22000-20000) = 18000
