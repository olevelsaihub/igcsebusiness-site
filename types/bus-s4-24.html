Mass marketing and niche marketing

Written

Mass marketing sells one product to the whole market. Niche marketing targets a small specialised segment. Mass brings big sales and low unit costs; niche brings higher prices and loyalty.

4

The method

  1. Broad or special?

    Whole market is mass; small segment is niche.

  2. Pick one benefit

    Mass: sales, low costs. Niche: price, loyalty.

  3. Link to customers

    How many are reached, or how well it fits.

  4. End on a result

    Revenue, profit or spread of risk.

  5. Apply it

    Link each point to the business's range of customers.

Do not lose marks here

  • ✗
    Swapped meanings

    Mass is the whole market.

  • ✗
    'Lots of advertising'

    Mass means whole-market selling.

  • ✗
    Defining

    Give an advantage.

  • ✗
    No result

    Follow it to sales or profit.

After your attempt, see the full worked explanation

Worked explanation

  1. Command: Explain one advantage, 3 marks; no definition needed.
  2. Advantage: more sales can be generated.
  3. Expand 1: the product is promoted to a very large number of people.
  4. Expand 2: higher sales raise revenue and can lower unit costs, improving profit.
Model answer · written by olevels.com, not an official Pearson mark scheme

Mass marketing can generate a very high volume of sales. Because the same product is promoted to the whole market rather than to one small group, a very large number of people become aware of it and may buy it. Selling in such large quantities increases revenue and allows the business to produce on a large scale, which can lower its cost per unit and improve its profit.

How the marks are earned

1 mark for the advantage (high sales). 1 mark for the link (promoted to a very large number of people). 1 mark for the result (more revenue and lower unit costs, so better profit).
