Calculate the current ratio

Calculation

Current ratio = current assets ÷ current liabilities, written as x:1. Below 1 means current assets cannot cover short-term debts.

The calculation

Current ratio = current assets ÷ current liabilities

Written as x:1, for example 1.75:1.

Tap a part to see what it means and how to find it.

5

The method

  1. Find the two totals

    Current assets and current liabilities, for the right year.

  2. Divide

    Current assets ÷ current liabilities.

  3. Round and write x:1

    Follow the rounding asked.

Do not lose marks here

  • ✗
    Upside down

    Assets go on top.

  • ✗
    Taking off inventory

    That gives the acid test, not this ratio.

  • ✗
    Subtracting

    A ratio is a division.

  • ✗
    Wrong rounding

    Follow the instruction exactly.

After your attempt, see the full worked explanation

Worked explanation

  1. Current assets = €26 000. Current liabilities = €14 852.
  2. Current ratio = 26 000 ÷ 14 852 = 1.7506…
  3. To two decimal places: 1.75, written as 1.75:1.
Model answer · written by olevels.com, not an official Pearson mark scheme

Current ratio = current assets ÷ current liabilities = 26 000 ÷ 14 852 = 1.75 (1.75:1).

How the marks are earned

Mark 1: 26 000 ÷ 14 852 set up. Mark 2: 1.75. The correct ratio alone earns both.

Calculation check

  • 26000 ÷ 14852 = 1.75
