Knowledge card

Statement of financial position: assets and liabilities

Written

The statement of financial position shows what a business owns (assets) and owes (liabilities) on one date. Sales and profit are in the income statement, not here.

Does it score?

Exam question

Explain one reason why a business owner uses a statement of financial position. (3 marks)

Decide first: would this answer earn the mark? Then tap it to check.

Tap an answer to see if it earns the mark.

4

The method

  1. Find the word

    Own means assets. Owe means liabilities.

  2. Apply the year rule

    Over a year is non-current. Within a year is current.

  3. Check the item

    Buildings and vehicles are non-current assets.

  4. Sales or profit?

    If it is a sales or profit figure, it is not here.

  5. Purpose

    Say what it shows, then who uses it.

Do not lose marks here

  • ✗
    Building as current

    Buildings, vehicles and factories are non-current assets.

  • ✗
    Twelve-month rule

    Mixing up current and non-current liabilities.

  • ✗
    Sales here

    Sales and gross profit belong in the income statement.

  • ✗
    Defining

    A definition earns nothing when a purpose is asked.

Worked explanation

  1. Command: Explain one purpose, 3 marks: one purpose plus two links.
  2. Purpose: shows whether the business can pay debts due soon.
  3. Link 1: managers compare current assets with current liabilities.
  4. Link 2: if short, they act early, e.g. reduce inventory or arrange finance.
Model answer · written by olevels.com, not an official Pearson mark scheme

One purpose of the statement of financial position is to show whether a business will be able to pay the debts it owes in the near future. Managers can compare its current assets, such as cash and inventory, with its current liabilities, such as money owed to suppliers. If the liabilities are larger, they can act early, for example by holding less inventory or arranging extra finance, before suppliers stop giving the business credit.

How the marks are earned

1 mark for the purpose (shows whether short-term debts can be paid). 1 mark for the first link (comparing current assets with current liabilities). 1 mark for the second link (early action such as cutting inventory or arranging finance).
