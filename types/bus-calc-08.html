Change between two values, absolute or percentage

Finding the change between an earlier and a later figure, either as an absolute difference in original units or as a percentage increase or decrease of the original.

Type 10 Calculations BUS-CALC-08

★★★★★ · 16 of 32 eligible examination papers, 2019–2025. Difficulty: Medium.

Gives two figures for different times (years, before and after an offer) and asks for the increase, decrease or percentage change.

Knowledge card

Change between two values, absolute or percentage

Calculation

Change = new − old. For a percentage, divide the change by the old value, then × 100. A fall is a decrease, even if the working gives a minus.

The calculation

% change = (new − old) ÷ old × 100

Absolute change = new value − old value.

Tap a part to see what it means and how to find it.

To find Absolute change:N − O

To find Decrease:give the fall as a positive %

Try itCustomers fall from 640 to 480. Percentage change?

  1. Change = 480 − 640 = −160
  2. 160 ÷ 640 × 100 = 25% decrease

The method

  1. Old and new

    Pick the right years and row.

  2. Subtract

    New − old.

  3. Divide by old

    Then × 100 for a percentage.

  4. Round, label

    Say increase or decrease.

Do not lose marks here

  • ✗
    Dividing by new

    Always use the old value.

  • ✗
    No subtraction

    New ÷ old is not a change.

  • ✗
    Not a percent

    Giving the plain difference.

  • ✗
    Wrong row

    Using the wrong years.

After your attempt, see the full worked explanation

Worked explanation

  1. Old value (2020–2021) = 85 083 thousand rupees. New value (2021–2022) = 92 322 thousand rupees.
  2. Change = 92 322 − 85 083 = 7 239.
  3. Percentage change = 7 239 ÷ 85 083 × 100 = 8.508…%.
  4. To two decimal places: 8.51%. It is an increase.
Model answer · written by olevels.com, not an official Pearson mark scheme

Increase = 92 322 − 85 083 = 7 239. Percentage increase = 7 239 ÷ 85 083 × 100 = 8.51%.

How the marks are earned

Mark 1: the difference 7 239 (or putting it over the original value). Mark 2: 8.51% to two decimal places. The correct percentage alone earns both.

Calculation check

  • 92322-85083 = 7239
  • (92322-85083) ÷ 85083 × 100 = 8.51
