Reasons why businesses fail

Written

A business fails when it cannot pay its debts and has to close. Typical causes: cash-flow problems, lack of finance, losing competitiveness and not adapting to change.

4

The method

  1. Name the cause

    Cash flow, finance, competition or not adapting.

  2. Explain it

    Say what it does inside the business: cash runs out or sales fall.

  3. Finish the chain

    End with unpaid bills or debts, losses and closure.

  4. Use the case

    Refer to the named business, not businesses in general.

Do not lose marks here

  • ✗
    Just a list

    Several causes with no explanation earn one mark.

  • ✗
    Stopping early

    Show why the business cannot carry on.

  • ✗
    Cash is not profit

    A profitable business can still fail if it runs out of cash.

Written-answer check
After your attempt, see the full worked explanation

Worked explanation

  1. Read the command: Explain one reason, 3 marks.
  2. Choose the clearest cause: cash-flow problems.
  3. Say what happens: outflows are bigger than inflows.
  4. Finish the chain: bills and wages cannot be paid, leading to closure.
Model answer · written by olevels.com, not an official Pearson mark scheme

A business may fail because of cash-flow problems. This happens when more cash flows out of the business than flows into it over a period, so the money in its bank account runs down. The business then cannot pay its suppliers, its workers' wages or its loan repayments on time, so creditors may take legal action and the business can be forced to close.

How the marks are earned

1 mark for the reason (cash-flow problems). 1 mark for explaining it (outflows larger than inflows). 1 mark for the consequence (cannot pay suppliers and workers, so it may close). Total 3.
