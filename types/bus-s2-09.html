Recruitment documents: job description to CV

Written

The job description is about the job. The person specification is about the person. Both are written by the business. The CV is written by the applicant.

The method

  1. Name the document

    Say who produces it.

  2. State the contents

    Tasks, skills, facts or history?

  3. Link to the job

    How does it help to match the right person?

  4. Carry on

    End with less training or fewer poor hires.

Do not lose marks here

  • ✗
    Mixing two

    Job = job description. Person = person specification.

  • ✗
    Wrong writer

    The applicant writes the CV only.

    Describing only

    Explain how it helps hiring.

Written-answer check
After your attempt, see the full worked explanation

Worked explanation

  1. Analyse card: two developed chains, each applied to Samsung.
  2. Pull context: skilled engineers, innovative TVs and smartphones, competition with Apple and Xiaomi, 270,000 staff.
  3. Chain 1: person specification sets required skills, so unsuitable people do not apply and HR time is saved.
  4. Chain 2: CVs and application forms let Samsung compare experience fairly and pick the strongest engineer, which supports innovation and competitiveness.
Model answer · written by olevels.com, not an official Pearson mark scheme

Samsung can use a person specification to set out the qualifications and experience it needs, such as an engineering degree and experience of designing smartphones or televisions. People who do not have these can see they are unsuitable and are less likely to apply. This means Samsung's human resources staff spend less time reading weak applications and can focus on the strongest candidates for its skilled engineering jobs.

Samsung can also compare applicants' CVs and application forms against the job description. Because an application form asks every applicant the same questions, Samsung can fairly compare each person's design experience and qualifications side by side. This makes it more likely that Samsung appoints the engineers with the most relevant skills, who can design the innovative products it needs to keep competing with Apple and Xiaomi in the smartphone market.

How the marks are earned

Levels-marked: AO2 (3) for applying documents to Samsung's engineers and products throughout; AO3 (3) for two logical chains, from document contents to fairer comparison or fewer weak applicants, to better hires and competitiveness. Detailed context in both chains reaches Level 3 (5-6).
