Knowledge card

Place: distribution channels and e-commerce

Written

Place is how and where customers can buy. A distribution channel is the route from producer to customer, through retailers or online. E-commerce reaches more people but has set-up costs.

4

The method

  1. Name the channel

    Own shop, other retailers, website or app.

  2. Pick one point

    Reach, cost, convenience, or a concern.

  3. Link it

    Who can now buy, or which costs change.

  4. End on a result

    Revenue, profit or market share.

  5. Apply it

    Tie it to the case's shop, products and customers.

Do not lose marks here

  • ✗
    Promotion mix-up

    Selling online is place; adverts are promotion.

  • ✗
    Definition only

    Give an advantage, not a meaning.

  • ✗
    Place = shop site

    Place includes online channels.

  • ✗
    Ignoring concerns

    Mention cost and customers who will not download.

Written-answer check
After your attempt, see the full worked explanation

Worked explanation

  1. Command: Explain one advantage, 3 marks; no definition.
  2. Advantage: lower costs.
  3. Expand 1: fewer shops or offices are needed because customers order online.
  4. Expand 2: lower overheads mean more profit from each sale.
Model answer · written by olevels.com, not an official Pearson mark scheme

Selling through e-commerce can reduce a business's costs. Because customers can order and pay on a website or app, the business does not need to rent and staff as many shops or offices. With lower running costs, more of the money from each sale is kept as profit, so the business's profit can increase.

How the marks are earned

1 mark for the advantage (lower costs). 1 mark for why (fewer premises and staff needed). 1 mark for the result (higher profit).
