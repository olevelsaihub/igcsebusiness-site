Trading blocs and tariffs

What a trading bloc is and how bloc membership and tariffs affect countries and businesses that import or export.

Type 9 1 Business activity and influences on business BUS-S1-21

★★☆☆☆ · 6 of 32 eligible examination papers, 2019–2025. Difficulty: Medium.

The question mentions a trade or trading bloc, tariffs, free trade, or importing between countries with trade barriers.

Knowledge

  • A trading bloc is a group of countries that agree to remove trade barriers, such as tariffs, between themselves.
  • A tariff is a tax on imported goods, which makes them more expensive in the importing country.
  • Inside a bloc, goods move between members without tariffs, so trade is cheaper and easier.
  • Blocs often keep tariffs on goods from non-member countries to protect their own businesses.
  • Benefits to a country: cheaper imports, more choice for consumers, bigger markets for its exporters, more jobs.

Before you begin

  • What imports and exports are
  • What a tax is

Specification: 1.4.1 · 1.6.2

Answer technique: Define a business term (1 mark) · Explain: point plus two linked expansions (3 marks)

Method

  1. Recall the two ideas: blocs remove barriers between members; tariffs are taxes on imports. Why: The correct trade feature earns the first mark.
  2. Decide whose side is asked: the country, a business inside the bloc, or an importer. Why: The effects differ by viewpoint.
  3. Link the feature to prices or costs. Why: No tariff means lower cost; a tariff means higher cost.
  4. Finish with the outcome: sales, choice, profit or jobs. Why: The last mark comes from the consequence.

How each command word is answered

Command wordTimes askedWhat to write
Define2Trade bloc: a group of countries that trade with each other free of barriers such as tariffs.
Explain4One feature plus two linked steps, for example no tariffs, lower costs, lower prices and more sales. No marks for a definition.

Common mistakes

Inferred, not measured: Inferred from the official schemes' credit and rejection patterns; not measured student-error statistics.

  • Defining a trading bloc when asked for an advantage, which earns no marks.
  • Saying tariffs make imports cheaper; they make them dearer.
  • Listing advantages without explaining any of them.
  • Writing about the business when the question asks about the country.

Worked explanation

  1. Read the command: Explain one advantage for a country, 3 marks; no definition needed.
  2. Pick one advantage: no tariffs on trade with members.
  3. Link: no extra tax cost on the goods.
  4. Finish: lower prices, more sales, more jobs for the country.
Model answer · written by olevels.com, not an official Pearson mark scheme

One advantage is that businesses in the country can sell to other member countries without paying tariffs. A tariff is a tax on imports, so removing it means goods traded between members carry no extra tax cost. This keeps the price of the country's exports lower in the other member countries, so its businesses can sell more, which raises output and jobs in the country.

How the marks are earned

1 mark for the advantage (trade without tariffs). 1 mark for the link (no extra tax cost on goods). 1 mark for the consequence (lower prices, more sales and jobs). Total 3.
