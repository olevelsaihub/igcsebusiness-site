Trading blocs and tariffs

Written

A trading bloc is a group of countries that remove trade barriers between themselves. A tariff is a tax on imports, so it makes imported goods dearer.

  1. No tariff
  2. Bigger market

No tariff

Why it matters
Members agree to remove tariffs between themselves.
Write it as
Goods move between members without tariffs.

Bigger market

Why it matters
Cheaper trade means more sales for the exporter.
Write it as
Its exporters sell more, so more jobs are created.

  1. Non-member
  2. 15% tariff

Non-member

Why it matters
Blocs often keep tariffs on non-members to protect their own firms.

15% tariff

Working
15% × $200 = $30
Meaning
A tax paid on the imported goods.

Price goes up

Working
$200 + $30 = $230
Effect
Imports cost more, so local makers may sell more.

Exam tipA tariff makes imports dearer, never cheaper.

4

The method

  1. Recall the idea

    Blocs remove barriers between members. A tariff is a tax on imports.

  2. Pick the viewpoint

    Is it the country, an exporter or an importer?

  3. Link to price

    No tariff means lower cost. A tariff means a higher price.

  4. Finish

    End with sales, choice, profit or jobs.

Do not lose marks here

  • ✗
    Wrong direction

    Tariffs raise the price of imports.

  • ✗
    Defining only

    If asked for an advantage, a definition scores nothing.

  • ✗
    Listing

    Explain each point, do not just list them.

Worked explanation

  1. Read the command: Explain one advantage for a country, 3 marks; no definition needed.
  2. Pick one advantage: no tariffs on trade with members.
  3. Link: no extra tax cost on the goods.
  4. Finish: lower prices, more sales, more jobs for the country.
Model answer · written by olevels.com, not an official Pearson mark scheme

One advantage is that businesses in the country can sell to other member countries without paying tariffs. A tariff is a tax on imports, so removing it means goods traded between members carry no extra tax cost. This keeps the price of the country's exports lower in the other member countries, so its businesses can sell more, which raises output and jobs in the country.

How the marks are earned

1 mark for the advantage (trade without tariffs). 1 mark for the link (no extra tax cost on goods). 1 mark for the consequence (lower prices, more sales and jobs). Total 3.
