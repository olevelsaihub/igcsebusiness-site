Calculate the operating profit margin

Calculation

Operating profit margin % = operating profit ÷ revenue × 100. Operating profit is gross profit − other operating expenses, so the margin is always lower than the gross profit margin.

The share of each $ of sales left after cost of sales and overheads.

To find Operating profit:GP − E

Try itRevenue $600 000, gross profit $240 000, other operating expenses $150 000. Find the operating profit margin.

  1. Operating profit = $240 000 − $150 000 = $90 000
  2. Margin = $90 000 ÷ $600 000 × 100 = 15.00%

The method

  1. Find operating profit

    Gross profit − other operating expenses.

  2. Find revenue

    The bottom of the fraction.

  3. Divide and × 100

    Operating profit ÷ revenue × 100.

  4. Round and label

    Add % and the rounding asked.

Do not lose marks here

  • ✗
    Using gross profit

    The top must be operating profit.

  • ✗
    Dividing by gross profit

    Revenue goes underneath.

  • ✗
    Expenses off revenue

    Take them off gross profit.

  • ✗
    Margin higher than gross

    Check again: it must be lower.

Worked explanation

  1. From Figure 1: revenue €330 606, gross profit €80 282, other operating expenses €12 692.
  2. Operating profit = 80 282 − 12 692 = €67 590.
  3. Operating profit margin = 67 590 ÷ 330 606 × 100 = 20.444…%.
  4. To two decimal places: 20.44%.
Model answer · written by olevels.com, not an official Pearson mark scheme

Operating profit = 80 282 − 12 692 = €67 590. Operating profit margin = 67 590 ÷ 330 606 × 100 = 20.44%.

How the marks are earned

Mark 1: operating profit of €67 590 (or the ratio set up). Mark 2: 20.44%. The correct answer alone earns both.
