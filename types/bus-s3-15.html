Knowledge card

Fixed and variable costs

Written

Fixed costs stay the same whatever the output; variable costs change as output changes. Test it: would this bill rise if the business made one more unit?

1

Fixed or variable?

Decide first, then tap the ? to check.

4 costs to find

Decide which costs are fixed and which are variable.

  1. Rent is fixed
  2. Fruit is variable
  3. Cups are variable
  4. Salary is fixed

Rent is fixed

Why
The same bill whether he sells 10 or 1 000 juices.

Fruit is variable

Why
Sell more juice and he buys more fruit.

Cups are variable

Why
Each drink needs a cup, so the bill rises with output.

Exam tipPackaging and raw materials are classic variable costs.

Salary is fixed

Why
A manager's pay does not change with output.
0 of 4 found

3 costs to find

Spot which cost changes and which one stays put.

  1. Wood: variable
  2. Rent: fixed
  3. Total cost

Wood: variable

Pattern
Output doubled, wood doubled.

Rent: fixed

Pattern
900 at every level of output.

Total cost

Working
1 500 + 900 = 2 400
Rule
Total cost = fixed + variable.

4

The method

  1. Ask the test

    Would this bill rise if one more unit were made?

  2. No means fixed

    Rent, insurance and managers' salaries stay the same.

  3. Yes means variable

    Raw materials, packaging and parts rise with output.

  4. Fit the business

    Choose a cost that suits the business named.

  5. Total

    Add fixed and variable for total cost.

Do not lose marks here

  • ✗
    Never changes

    A fixed cost does not change with output. It can still change over time.

  • ✗
    Packaging as fixed

    Packaging and materials are variable.

  • ✗
    Wrong business

    A salon does not buy steel. Pick a cost that fits.

  • ✗
    Mixing in other costs

    Do not call an overdraft charge or commission a fixed cost.

After your attempt, see the full worked explanation

Worked explanation

  1. Command: Define, 1 mark: one clear sentence.
  2. Key words: costs, change, output.
  3. Add an example only to support the meaning, not to replace it.
Model answer · written by olevels.com, not an official Pearson mark scheme

Variable costs are costs whose total changes when the amount of output changes, for example the cost of the flowers bought rises as it makes more arrangements.

How the marks are earned

1 mark for stating that the cost changes as output changes. The example supports the answer but does not earn a separate mark.
