Average (mean) per unit or outlet

Finding a mean by dividing a total by the number of outlets or units.

Type 1 Calculations BUS-CALC-19

★☆☆☆☆ · 1 of 32 eligible examination papers, 2019–2025. Difficulty: Easy.

Asks for the average number per shop, showroom or unit and gives a total and a count.

See frequency evidence and how ratings work

Main plan: Top in the World Plan · Further topics: dismissal, averages and productivity

KnowledgeMethodWorked examplePracticeTeach it back

Knowledge

  • The mean (average) = total ÷ number of items (shops, showrooms, units).
  • Write large numbers in full before dividing: 9 million = 9 000 000.
  • The question may include extra figures (employees, turnover); use only the total and the count it asks about.
  • Counts of things are given as whole numbers.

Before you begin

  • Writing millions and billions in full
  • Division

Answer technique: Set out a calculation with working (2 marks)

Method

  1. Find the total that is being shared out. Why: This is the top of the division.
  2. Find the number of outlets or units. Why: This is what you divide by.
  3. Divide total by number and give the answer with its unit. Why: This is the mean.

Common mistakes

Inferred, not measured: Inferred from the official schemes' credit and rejection patterns; not measured student-error statistics.

  • Using the wrong figure, such as employees or turnover, instead of the sales total.
  • Mistakes writing 'million' as a number (too few or too many zeros).
  • Dividing the number of outlets by the total.

Hint for part (a)

Find the total and the count the question asks about, and ignore extra figures. Write millions in full before sharing the total equally, and give a whole number for counts.

vehicles

Worked explanation

  1. Total cars sold = 9 million = 9 000 000. Number of showrooms = 6 000.
  2. The 60,000 employees and $42 billion turnover are not needed.
  3. Average = 9 000 000 ÷ 6 000 = 1 500.

Average vehicles sold per showroom = 9 000 000 ÷ 6 000 = 1 500 vehicles.

How the marks are earned
