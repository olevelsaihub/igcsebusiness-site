Average (mean) per unit or outlet

  2. 7 500 000 ÷ 25 = 300 000 tickets

The method

  1. Find the total

    The figure being shared out.

  2. Find the number

    How many shops or units.

  3. Divide

    Total ÷ number, with the unit.

Do not lose marks here

  • ✗
    Wrong figure

    Do not use staff or turnover unless asked.

  • ✗
    Zeros

    Write million as 1 000 000.

  • ✗
    Upside down

    The total goes on top.

Average (mean) per unit or outlet · June 2019 · Paper 2 · Question 4 · part (a) · 2 marks

(a) Calculate the average number of vehicles sold in each car showroom. You are advised to show your workings. (2)

View full original question and diagrams
Hint for part (a)

Find the total and the count the question asks about, and ignore extra figures. Write millions in full before sharing the total equally, and give a whole number for counts.

Your answer

Work it out on paper, then type your final answer. Give it to 0 decimal places.

Final-value check
vehicles
After your attempt, see the full worked explanation

Worked explanation

  1. Total cars sold = 9 million = 9 000 000. Number of showrooms = 6 000.
  2. The 60,000 employees and $42 billion turnover are not needed.
  3. Average = 9 000 000 ÷ 6 000 = 1 500.
Model answer · written by olevels.com, not an official Pearson mark scheme

Average vehicles sold per showroom = 9 000 000 ÷ 6 000 = 1 500 vehicles.

How the marks are earned

Mark 1: 9 000 000 ÷ 6 000 set up. Mark 2: 1 500 vehicles. The correct answer alone earns both.

  • 9000000 ÷ 6000 = 1500
