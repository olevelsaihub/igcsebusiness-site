Total cost from fixed and variable costs

Finding total cost by adding fixed cost to variable cost per unit (or per hour) multiplied by output or time.

Type 6 Calculations BUS-CALC-03

★★☆☆☆ · 7 of 32 eligible examination papers, 2019–2025. Difficulty: Easy.

Asks for 'total cost' or 'total monthly cost' and gives fixed costs plus a cost per unit, per item or per hour.

Knowledge card

Total cost from fixed and variable costs

Calculation

Total cost = fixed costs + (variable cost per unit × units). Fixed costs stay the same; variable costs rise with output. The selling price is not a cost.

The calculation

Total cost = fixed costs + (variable cost per unit × units)

Total variable cost = variable cost per unit × units.

Tap a part to see what it means and how to find it.

To find Total variable cost:VC × Q

To find Fixed costs:TC − (VC × Q)

Try itFixed costs $3 500 a month, variable cost $4 a unit, 900 units, selling price $9. Total cost?

  1. Variable = 900 × $4 = $3 600
  2. Total = $3 500 + $3 600 = $7 100

5

The method

  1. Pick out figures

    Fixed cost, variable cost per unit, units. Ignore price.

  2. Variable total

    Cost per unit × units.

  3. Add fixed

    Total cost needs both.

  4. Check period

    Month with month, week with week.

Do not lose marks here

  • ✗
    One unit only

    Adding fixed cost to the cost of one unit.

  • ✗
    Using the price

    Selling price is not a cost.

  • ✗
    No fixed cost

    Forgetting to add it.

  • ✗
    Mixed periods

    Weekly with monthly figures.

Worked explanation

  1. From Figure 1: fixed costs 555.31 SGD, variable cost per unit 0.93 SGD, output 160 toy carts.
  2. Total variable cost = 0.93 × 160 = 148.80 SGD.
  3. Total cost = 555.31 + 148.80 = 704.11 SGD.
Model answer · written by olevels.com, not an official Pearson mark scheme

Total cost = fixed costs + (variable cost per unit × output) = 555.31 + (0.93 × 160) = 555.31 + 148.80 = 704.11 SGD.

How the marks are earned

Mark 1: putting the right numbers into fixed cost + variable cost per unit × output. Mark 2: the correct total of 704.11 SGD. The correct total on its own earns both marks.

Calculation check

  • 0.93 × 160 = 148.8
  • 555.31+0.93 × 160 = 704.11
