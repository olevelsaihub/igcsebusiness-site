Calculate the gross profit margin

Calculation

Gross profit margin % = gross profit ÷ revenue × 100. Gross profit is revenue − cost of sales. Always divide by revenue.

The calculation

Gross profit margin % = gross profit ÷ revenue × 100

The share of each $1 of sales kept as gross profit.

Tap a part to see what it means and how to find it.

To find Gross profit:R − CoS

Try itRevenue $250 000, cost of sales $175 000. Find the gross profit margin.

  1. Gross profit = $250 000 − $175 000 = $75 000
  2. Margin = $75 000 ÷ $250 000 × 100 = 30.00%

5

The method

  1. Find gross profit

    If missing: revenue − cost of sales.

  2. Find revenue

    Same year, same column.

  3. Divide and × 100

    Gross profit ÷ revenue × 100.

  4. Round and label

    Add % and follow the rounding asked.

Do not lose marks here

  • ✗
    Dividing by cost of sales

    Revenue is always underneath.

  • ✗
    Upside down

    Gross profit goes on top.

  • ✗
    Wrong column

    Check the year heading before you copy.

  • ✗
    No rounding

    Give the decimal places asked.

Worked explanation

  1. From Figure 2: sales revenue €19 220, gross profit €4 500.
  2. Gross profit margin = 4 500 ÷ 19 220 × 100 = 23.413…%.
  3. To 2 decimal places: 23.41%.
Model answer · written by olevels.com, not an official Pearson mark scheme

Gross profit margin = gross profit ÷ revenue × 100 = 4 500 ÷ 19 220 × 100 = 23.41%.

How the marks are earned

Mark 1: 4 500 ÷ 19 220 × 100 set up. Mark 2: 23.41%. The correct answer alone earns both.
