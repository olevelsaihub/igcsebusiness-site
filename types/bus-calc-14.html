Business / Question types / Calculate the gross profit margin

Pearson Edexcel · International GCSE Business · 4BS1

Calculate the gross profit margin

Calculating gross profit margin as gross profit divided by revenue times 100, finding gross profit first if needed.

Type 4 Calculations BUS-CALC-14

★★★☆☆ · 10 of 32 eligible examination papers, 2019–2025. Difficulty: Easy.

Asks for the gross profit margin, usually from a statement of comprehensive income extract with revenue, cost of sales and gross profit.

Knowledge

  • Gross profit = revenue (sales) − cost of sales. Cost of sales is the direct cost of making or buying the goods sold.
  • Gross profit margin = gross profit ÷ revenue × 100.
  • It shows how many cents of gross profit the business keeps from each unit of currency of sales.
  • Pearson provides the ratio formulae in the exam, but you must know which figures go where and how to round.
  • Usually give the answer as a percentage to 2 decimal places.
  • Figures in thousands or millions are fine as long as both figures use the same unit.

Before you begin

  • Revenue and cost of sales
  • Converting a fraction to a percentage

Learn these types first: Work out profit or loss

Specification: 3.5.1

Answer technique: Set out a calculation with working (2 marks)

Method

  1. Find gross profit; if it is not given, work it out as revenue − cost of sales. Why: The top of the formula is gross profit.
  2. Find revenue (sales) for the same business or column. Why: The bottom of the formula is revenue.
  3. Divide gross profit by revenue and multiply by 100. Why: This gives the margin as a percentage.
  4. Round as asked and add the % sign. Why: Rounding is part of the second mark.

How each command word is answered

Command wordTimes askedWhat to write
Calculate10Use the Calculate card: write gross profit ÷ revenue × 100 with the figures, then the percentage to 2 decimal places.

Common mistakes

Inferred, not measured: Inferred from the official schemes' credit and rejection patterns; not measured student-error statistics.

  • Dividing by cost of sales instead of revenue.
  • Dividing revenue by gross profit (upside down).
  • Taking figures from the wrong column of a multi-column table.
  • Not rounding to the decimal places asked.

Worked explanation

  1. From Figure 2: sales revenue €19 220, gross profit €4 500.
  2. Gross profit margin = 4 500 ÷ 19 220 × 100 = 23.413…%.
  3. To 2 decimal places: 23.41%.
Model answer · written by olevels.com, not an official Pearson mark scheme

Gross profit margin = gross profit ÷ revenue × 100 = 4 500 ÷ 19 220 × 100 = 23.41%.

How the marks are earned

Mark 1: 4 500 ÷ 19 220 × 100 set up. Mark 2: 23.41%. The correct answer alone earns both.
