Knowledge card

Meaning of stakeholder and shareholder

Written

A stakeholder is any person or group with an interest in, or affected by, a business. A shareholder owns shares in a company. Every shareholder is a stakeholder, but not every stakeholder is a shareholder.

1

Stakeholder or shareholder?

Decide first, then tap the ? to check.

3 features to find

Decide which groups are stakeholders and why.

  1. Employees
  2. Local community
  3. Lender

Employees

Why
They are affected by pay and whether the bakery survives.

Local community

Why
They are affected even though they buy and own nothing.

Exam tipStakeholders do not have to own anything.

Lender

Why
The bank has an interest: it wants its money back.

3 features to find

Find the shareholders. Who is only a stakeholder?

  1. Ana owns shares
  2. Supplier: not a shareholder
  3. Employee: not a shareholder

Ana owns shares

Meaning
She is a part-owner of the company.
Write it as
A shareholder owns one or more shares in a company.

Supplier: not a shareholder

Meaning
It is a stakeholder (it wants to be paid) but owns no shares.

Employee: not a shareholder

Meaning
Mei is a stakeholder because the business affects her job.

Exam tipDo not define a shareholder as someone who just 'invests'. Say they own shares.

4

The method

  1. Which term?

    Decide whether it asks for stakeholder or shareholder.

  2. Stakeholder

    Say a person or group with an interest in, or affected by, the business.

  3. Shareholder

    Say someone who owns shares in a company.

  4. Add an example

    Optional: one short example after the meaning.

Do not lose marks here

  • ✗
    Owner or interest

    A stakeholder does not need to own the business.

  • ✗
    Example only

    'Customers' is not a meaning.

  • ✗
    Vague 'invests'

    A shareholder owns shares; say so.

Worked explanation

  1. Read the command: Define, 1 mark.
  2. Give the meaning in one sentence: a person or group with an interest in the business.
  3. Add a short example to show understanding.
Model answer · written by olevels.com, not an official Pearson mark scheme

A stakeholder is any person or group that is affected by, or has an interest in, the activities of a business, for example its employees, customers or the local community.

How the marks are earned

1 mark for a correct meaning that shows the person or group has an interest in, or is affected by, the business.
