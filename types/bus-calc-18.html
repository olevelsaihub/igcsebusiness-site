Knowledge card

Calculate the acid test ratio

Calculation

Acid test ratio = (current assets − inventory) ÷ current liabilities. Inventory may take time to sell, so it is left out. This is a stricter test than the current ratio.

The calculation

Acid test ratio = (current assets − inventory) ÷ current liabilities

A stricter test than the current ratio. Written as x:1.

Tap a part to see what it means and how to find it.

Try itCurrent assets $54 000; inventory $24 000; current liabilities $20 000. Find the acid test ratio.

  1. Without inventory: $54 000 − $24 000 = $30 000
  2. Ratio = $30 000 ÷ $20 000 = 1.50:1

The method

  1. Take out inventory

    Current assets − inventory.

  2. Divide

    Divide by current liabilities.

  3. Round and write x:1

    Follow the rounding asked.

Do not lose marks here

  • ✗
    Forgetting inventory

    That gives the current ratio.

  • ✗
    Inventory off liabilities

    Take it off current assets.

  • ✗
    No brackets

    Subtract first, then divide.

Worked explanation

  1. From Figure 3 (TRY million): current assets 12, inventory 4, current liabilities 7.
  2. Current assets − inventory = 12 − 4 = 8.
  3. Acid test ratio = 8 ÷ 7 = 1.1428…
  4. To two decimal places: 1.14, written as 1.14:1.
Model answer · written by olevels.com, not an official Pearson mark scheme

Acid test ratio = (current assets − inventory) ÷ current liabilities = (12 − 4) ÷ 7 = 8 ÷ 7 = 1.14 (1.14:1).

How the marks are earned

Mark 1: (12 − 4) or 8 ÷ 7 set up. Mark 2: 1.14. The correct answer alone earns both.

Calculation check

  • 12-4 = 8
  • (12-4) ÷ 7 = 1.14
