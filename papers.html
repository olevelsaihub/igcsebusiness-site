Past papers and grade guide

Official question papers to sit under exam conditions, and the grade your marks reach.

Every official 4BS1 question paper from 2019 to 2025, main and regional (R). Print a full pair, Paper 1 and Paper 2 of the same option, sit it under timed conditions (1 hour 30 minutes each), then give it to your teacher to mark against the official mark scheme.

Official mark schemes are for teachers only. Students check individual parts with “Check my answer” on each question page.

Grade guide

After a full pair has been marked against the official scheme, enter the two marks to see the grade they reach on the official Pearson June 2026 boundaries for 4BS1 (out of 160). Main papers 01 + 02 and regional papers 01R + 02R have different boundaries. The guide also shows the grade that stays secure with a 10-mark teacher buffer, an internal caution margin, not a Pearson boundary.

A score on one past pair does not guarantee a future grade; use several fresh pairs, timed, and marked against the official scheme.
