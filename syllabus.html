Business / Syllabus

Pearson Edexcel · International GCSE Business · 4BS1

Syllabus

Every official statement and bullet, with the question types that cover it.

Source: Pearson Edexcel International GCSE Business specification, Issue 2 (2026-02). Statement codes and bullet wording are official; internal IDs for unnumbered bullets are not Pearson codes.

1. Business activity and influences on business

1.1 Business objectives

1.1.1 Businesses can have several objectives:

  • financial aims and objectives - survival, profit, sales, market share, financial security
  • non-financial aims and objectives - social objectives, personal satisfaction, challenge, independence and control.

19 past-paper parts assess this directly (13 main, 6 regional).

Question types: Business objectives: financial, non-financial and social · External factors affecting business decisions

1.1.2 Why business aims and objectives change as businesses evolve:

  • in response to market conditions, technology, performance, legislation, internal reasons.

1 past-paper parts assess this directly (1 main, 0 regional).

Question types: Business objectives: financial, non-financial and social

1.2 Types of organisations

1.2.1 The main types of business ownership:

  • sole trader
  • partnerships
  • limited companies (private and public)
  • public corporations.

25 past-paper parts assess this directly (17 main, 8 regional).

Question types: Forms of business ownership and limited liability

1.2.2 Characteristics relating to size:

  • concepts of risk, ownership and limited liability
  • public corporations – reasons for and against public ownership
  • ownership, control, sources of finance, use of profits, stakeholders and shareholders
  • appropriateness of different forms of ownership.

28 past-paper parts assess this directly (20 main, 8 regional).

Question types: Meaning of stakeholder and shareholder · Forms of business ownership and limited liability · Multinationals and globalisation

1.2.3 Different forms of business organisation:

  • franchises
  • social enterprises
  • multinationals.

17 past-paper parts assess this directly (13 main, 4 regional).

Question types: Social enterprises: meaning and purpose · Franchising: franchisor and franchisee · Multinationals and globalisation · Sources of finance and why businesses need finance

1.3 Classification of businesses

1.3.1 Primary, secondary and tertiary activities:

  • primary sector – extracting raw materials from the earth
  • secondary sector – converting raw materials into finished or semi-finished goods
  • tertiary sector – provision of a wide variety of services.

11 past-paper parts assess this directly (8 main, 3 regional).

Question types: Primary, secondary and tertiary sectors

1.4 Decisions on location

1.4.1 The main factors influencing location decisions and relocation of a business:

  • proximity to market, labour, materials and competitors
  • nature of the business activity
  • the impact of the internet on location decisions - e-commerce and/or fixed premises
  • legal controls and trade blocs.

14 past-paper parts assess this directly (10 main, 4 regional).

Question types: Trading blocs and tariffs · Choosing where to locate a business · Place: distribution channels and e-commerce

1.5 Business and the international economy

1.5.1 Globalisation:

  • concept of globalisation
  • opportunities and threats of globalisation for businesses.

3 past-paper parts assess this directly (2 main, 1 regional).

Question types: Multinationals and globalisation

1.5.2 The importance and growth of multinationals:

  • benefits of a business becoming a multinational
  • benefits to a country and/or economy where a multinational company is located
  • possible drawbacks to a country and/or economy where a multinational is located.

9 past-paper parts assess this directly (5 main, 4 regional).

Question types: Multinationals and globalisation

1.5.3 Exchange rate calculation.

24 past-paper parts assess this directly (14 main, 10 regional).

Question types: Convert prices between currencies with exchange rates

1.5.4 The impact of exchange rate changes:

  • on international competitiveness
  • on importers and exporters.

5 past-paper parts assess this directly (4 main, 1 regional).

Question types: Effects of exchange rate changes

1.6 Government objectives and policies

1.6.1 Government spending:

  • to provide public service
  • taxation and constraints on public spending.

4 past-paper parts assess this directly (3 main, 1 regional).

Question types: Forms of business ownership and limited liability · Government spending, taxation and laws · External factors affecting business decisions

1.6.2 How governments can affect business activity:

  • infrastructure provision
  • legislation
  • trade policy - membership of trading blocs, tariffs.

9 past-paper parts assess this directly (5 main, 4 regional).

Question types: Government spending, taxation and laws · Trading blocs and tariffs

1.6.3 The effect of interest rates on:

  • businesses
  • consumer spending.

12 past-paper parts assess this directly (7 main, 5 regional).

Question types: Effects of interest rate changes

1.7 External factors

1.7.1 The external factors affecting business decisions:

  • social
  • technological
  • environmental
  • political.

12 past-paper parts assess this directly (7 main, 5 regional).

Question types: Government spending, taxation and laws · External factors affecting business decisions · Place: distribution channels and e-commerce · Technology and robotics in production · Quality, quality control and competitive advantage

1.8 What makes a business successful?

1.8.1 Measuring success in business:

  • revenue
  • market share
  • customer satisfaction
  • profit
  • growth
  • owner/shareholder satisfaction
  • employee satisfaction.

18 past-paper parts assess this directly (9 main, 9 regional).

Question types: Measuring business success · Using income statements and financial documents to decide · Why marketing matters: needs, loyalty and share

1.8.2 Reasons for business failure:

  • cash flow problems/lack of finance
  • not competitive
  • failure to adapt to changes in the market.

6 past-paper parts assess this directly (3 main, 3 regional).

Question types: Reasons why businesses fail · Why cash matters and how to improve cash flow

2. People in Business

2.1 Internal and external communication

2.1.1 Importance of good communication and the problems of ineffective communication:

  • the different communication methods used, including information technology (IT) and the benefits and limitations of each method.

13 past-paper parts assess this directly (7 main, 6 regional).

Question types: Communication: methods, barriers and why it matters · Span of control, chain of command and delegation · Technology in promotion and social media

2.1.2 Barriers to communication:

  • how communication barriers arise
  • problems of ineffective communication and how they can be removed.

6 past-paper parts assess this directly (4 main, 2 regional).

Question types: Communication: methods, barriers and why it matters

2.2.1 Types of employment:

  • full-time
  • part-time
  • job share
  • casual/seasonal/temporary.

18 past-paper parts assess this directly (12 main, 6 regional).

Question types: Types of employment: full-time, part-time, temporary

2.2.2 Recruitment documents:

  • job description
  • person specification
  • application form
  • curriculum vitae (CV)/résumé.

14 past-paper parts assess this directly (9 main, 5 regional).

Question types: Recruitment documents: job description to CV

2.2.3 Internal and external recruitment:

  • job advertisement
  • shortlisting
  • interviewing.

17 past-paper parts assess this directly (12 main, 5 regional).

Question types: Functional areas and the human resources role · Recruitment documents: job description to CV · Internal and external recruitment · Selection: shortlisting and interviewing candidates

2.2.4 Legal controls over employment and their effects:

  • equal opportunities – gender, race, disability, religion, sexual preference, age
  • minimum wage laws.

16 past-paper parts assess this directly (9 main, 7 regional).

Question types: Government spending, taxation and laws · Equal opportunities and minimum wage laws · Why motivating and keeping staff matters

2.3 Training

2.3.1 Importance of training to a business and workers:

  • different types of training and the main purpose
  • induction
  • on-the-job
  • off-the-job
  • compliance with health and safety laws
  • benefits and limitations of training.

22 past-paper parts assess this directly (18 main, 4 regional).

Question types: Health and safety at work · Training: induction, on-the-job and off-the-job

2.4 Motivation and rewards

2.4.1 The importance of motivation in the workplace:

  • attracting employees, retaining employees, productivity
  • motivational theories of Herzberg, Maslow and Taylor.

30 past-paper parts assess this directly (20 main, 10 regional).

Question types: Measuring business success · Motivation theories: Maslow, Taylor and Herzberg · Why motivating and keeping staff matters · Types of employment: full-time, part-time, temporary · Financial and non-financial methods of motivation

2.4.2 How businesses motivate employees:

  • financial methods:
  • remuneration
  • bonus
  • commission
  • promotion
  • fringe benefits
  • non-financial methods:
  • job rotation
  • job enrichment
  • autonomy.

35 past-paper parts assess this directly (23 main, 12 regional).

Question types: Why motivating and keeping staff matters · Internal and external recruitment · Types of employment: full-time, part-time, temporary · Financial and non-financial methods of motivation

2.5 Organisation structure and employees

2.5.1 Organisational charts for different types of business:

  • hierarchical and flat
  • centralised and decentralised.

9 past-paper parts assess this directly (8 main, 1 regional).

Question types: Span of control, chain of command and delegation · Organisational structures: hierarchical, flat, centralised, decentralised

2.5.2 Roles and responsibilities of employees in terms of compliance and accountability:

  • span of control
  • chain of command
  • delegation.

14 past-paper parts assess this directly (11 main, 3 regional).

Question types: Span of control, chain of command and delegation · Organisational structures: hierarchical, flat, centralised, decentralised

2.5.3 The different functional areas within a business:

  • human resources – workforce planning, recruitment and selection, training, health and safety, staff welfare, employment issues, industrial relations, disciplinary and grievance procedure, dismissal, unfair dismissal and redundancy
  • finance – wages/salaries, cash-flow forecasting, budgets and accounting
  • marketing – market research, product planning, pricing, sales promotion, advertising, customer service, public relations, packaging and distribution
  • production – manufacturing the product, designing new products, quality control and stock control.

29 past-paper parts assess this directly (20 main, 9 regional).

Question types: Dismissing an employee · Functional areas and the human resources role · Health and safety at work · Statement of financial position: assets and liabilities · Why marketing matters: needs, loyalty and share · Lean production: JIT, Kaizen and using resources well
