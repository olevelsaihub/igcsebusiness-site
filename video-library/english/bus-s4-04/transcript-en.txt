Idea. Think of a new mobile game. Before launch it is designed and tested, so nobody can buy it yet. At first only a few people play it. Then everyone is talking about it. After a while sales level off, and finally people move on to something else. This pattern is called the product life cycle: the phases a product passes through from launch until it is withdrawn. From launch there are four phases, in order: introduction, growth, maturity and decline. Development happens before launch, so it is not counted as phase one.

Method. At introduction the product is new and few people know about it, so sales are low. In growth, more people hear about it and sales rise quickly. In maturity, most people who want the product already have it, so sales level off. In decline, customers move to newer products and sales fall. In the end, the product may be withdrawn. An extension strategy is an action that stops sales falling and makes the product last longer. It is usually used in maturity, before decline sets in. Common extension strategies are new packaging, a new feature or flavour, a lower price, selling to new markets or customers, and more advertising.

Worked example. Here is a real question from Paper one in June 2019. Explain one way the product life cycle can be extended. It is worth three marks: one strategy, then two linked steps. Mark one is the strategy: change the packaging, for example a brighter, more modern design. Mark two shows how customers react. The new look makes the product seem fresh, so existing customers are reminded of it and new customers notice it on the shelf. Mark three is the effect on sales. Repeat purchases and new buyers lift sales again, instead of letting them fall into decline.

Common mistake. A common mistake is mixing up the product life cycle with the Boston matrix. Star, cash cow, question mark and dog are Boston matrix words, not life cycle phases.

Your turn. Now you try. A juice company's orange drink has sold well for eight years, but sales have stopped growing. Which phase is it in? Explain one extension strategy for it. Pause here and work it out.

Answer. Sales have levelled off, so it is in maturity. One strategy is a new flavour, such as orange and mango. Customers try something new from a brand they know, so sales rise again. That is the method for this type: name one strategy, show how customers react, then give the effect on sales. Now try the practice questions.
