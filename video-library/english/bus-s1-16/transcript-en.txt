Think of a brand you can buy in almost any country, such as a big fast-food chain or a phone maker. A business that produces or sells in more than one country is called a multinational. Globalisation is the growth of trade and business links across the whole world. It brings opportunities, like selling to customers abroad online, and threats, like more foreign competition. Keep the two terms apart. Globalisation is a worldwide trend. A multinational is one business.

Step one: decide whose side the question takes. Is it the business itself, or the host country, where the multinational locates? The benefits are different. The host country can also lose out. Profits may be sent abroad, local firms may be squeezed out, and wages may be low or the environment harmed. Step two: pick one clear gain. Step three: chain it to an outcome: sales, costs and profit for a firm; jobs, taxes and living standards for a country.

Here is a real question from Paper two in November 2021, about Huawei. Explain one benefit of being a multinational business. It is worth three marks, and no definition is needed. Mark one is the benefit. Selling in many countries spreads the business's risk. Mark two explains why. Huawei sells smartphones in many markets, so if demand falls in one country, perhaps in a recession, sales elsewhere make up for it. Mark three gives the consequence. Total revenue stays steadier, so the business is less likely to face cash problems or cut production.

Mistake one: answering for the wrong side. If the question asks about the host country, the firm's extra sales are not the answer. Mistake two: defining multinational when the question asks for a benefit. The definition earns nothing in an explain answer.

Now you try. A car maker opens a new factory in a country called Valoria, employing two thousand local people. Explain one benefit to Valoria. Pause and decide.

The factory creates two thousand new jobs. Those workers earn wages and gain new skills. They spend more in local shops, and the government collects more tax to spend on public services. Remember the method: decide whose side, pick one clear gain, then chain it to an outcome. Now try the practice questions for this type.
