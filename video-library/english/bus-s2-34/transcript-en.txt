Idea. Think about your school. Teachers teach, the office collects fees and pays the bills, and someone organises events and tells parents about them. Each group does one type of work. A business works the same way. A part of a business that does one type of work is called a functional area, or department. The four main ones are human resources, finance, marketing and production.

Method. Human resources, or HR, deals with people. It plans how many staff are needed, recruits and selects them, trains them, and looks after health and safety, welfare, discipline and dismissal. Finance deals with money. It pays wages and suppliers, sends invoices, and prepares budgets and cash-flow forecasts. Marketing deals with customers: market research, pricing, advertising, promotion and customer service. Production makes the product: manufacturing, designing new products, quality control and stock control. To match a task to its department, ask one question. Is it about people, money, customers, or making the product? The answer tells you the department. In a State question, describe the task using the business's own products or staff. In an Explain question, name one responsibility, say what the department actually does, then give the result for the business.

Worked example. Here is a real question from Paper one R in June twenty twenty-four. Explain one responsibility of the human resources function. It is worth three marks: one responsibility, then two linked steps. Mark one names the responsibility. Human resources recruits and selects new employees. Mark two describes what HR actually does. It writes the job description and person specification, advertises the vacancy, shortlists the applicants and interviews them. Mark three gives the result. The business appoints the person whose skills best match the job, so they work well and are less likely to leave. This explanation is by olevels.com, not an official mark scheme.

Common mistake. A common mistake is saying that HR pays the wages. HR plans the staffing, but finance actually pays the money. Another mistake is putting quality control under marketing. Checking that products are made to the right standard is part of production.

Your turn. Now you try. A bakery chain has three tasks. Check that every loaf is the right weight. Pay the flour supplier. Train new shop assistants. Which department does each task? Pause and decide.

Answer. Checking the weight of each loaf is quality control, so it is production. Paying the flour supplier moves money, so it is finance. Training new shop assistants is about people, so it is human resources. Remember the method: find the task, ask whether it is about people, money, customers or making, then name the department. For Explain, add what it does and the result. Now try the practice questions.
