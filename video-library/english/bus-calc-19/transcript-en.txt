Idea. Imagine three friends sharing a bag of thirty sweets equally. Each friend gets ten. Sharing a total out equally is exactly what an average does. In Business, the average is called the mean. The mean equals the total divided by the number of items, such as shops, showrooms or units. Here is a business example. Four cafés sell twelve hundred, eighteen hundred, fourteen hundred and sixteen hundred cups. The total is six thousand. Six thousand divided by four is fifteen hundred cups per café.

Method. The method has three steps. Find the total being shared out. Find the number of outlets or units. Then divide the total by the number, and write the unit. Write large numbers in full before you divide. Nine million is nine followed by six zeros. Two point five million is two million, five hundred thousand. A billion has nine zeros. Exam cases often give extra figures, such as employees or turnover. Use only the total and the count the question asks about.

Worked example. Here is a real question from Paper two in June twenty nineteen. Calculate the average number of vehicles sold in each car showroom. It is worth two marks. The case gives four figures. We need vehicles sold, nine million, and showrooms, six thousand. The employees and the turnover are not needed. Write nine million in full, then divide by six thousand. Nine million divided by six thousand is fifteen hundred vehicles per showroom.

Common mistake. A common mistake is using the wrong figure, such as the employees. That gives ten, which is employees per showroom, not vehicles. Another mistake is dividing the wrong way round. A tiny answer like this is a warning sign. The total always goes on top.

Your turn. Now you try. A bakery chain sells two point four million loaves a year through three hundred shops. It employs eighteen hundred staff. Calculate the average number of loaves sold per shop. Pause here and work it out.

Answer. Two point four million is two million, four hundred thousand. Ignore the staff. Divide by three hundred shops. The answer is eight thousand loaves per shop. Remember the method: find the total, find the number, then divide and write the unit. Now try the practice questions for this type.
