Idea. You have probably seen a shop near you close down. One month it is open, the next month the shutters are down. Business failure means a business has to stop trading and close. Often the end comes when a business cannot pay its debts when they are due. This is called insolvency. The question is always: what caused it?

Method. There are four main causes. Cash-flow problems, when more cash goes out than comes in. Lack of finance, when it cannot raise the money it needs. Not being competitive, when rivals offer better prices, quality or service. And failing to adapt, when tastes or technology change and the business does not. To explain a cause, build a chain. Name the cause. Say what it does inside the business, such as cash running out or sales falling. Then finish at the end point: unpaid debts, losses, and closure. When the question names a business, use its facts. A shop with a new rival next door may lose competitiveness. A firm whose customers pay late may face cash-flow problems. Choose the cause the evidence points to.

Worked example. Here is a question from Pearson's Paper two, June twenty twenty-one. Explain one reason why businesses fail. It is worth three marks: one reason, then two linked steps. Mark one is the reason. A business may fail because of cash-flow problems. Mark two explains it. More cash flows out of the business than flows in, so the money in its bank account runs down. Mark three finishes the chain. The business cannot pay its suppliers, wages or loan repayments on time, so creditors may take legal action and it may be forced to close.

Common mistake. Mistake one: listing several causes with no explanation. A list earns only one mark. One cause, fully explained, earns all three. Mistake two: confusing cash with profit. A business can make a profit on paper and still fail, if customers pay late and it runs out of cash to pay its bills.

Your turn. Now you try. A video game shop only sells games on discs, but most customers now download their games. Explain one reason why this shop might fail. Pause and decide.

Answer. The shop has failed to adapt to a change in technology. Customers buy downloads instead, so its sales fall. With less money coming in, it makes losses and cannot pay its rent and suppliers, so it may have to close. That is the pattern for this type: name one cause, say what it does inside the business, then finish at closure. Now try the practice questions.
