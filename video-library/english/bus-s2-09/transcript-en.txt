Idea. Imagine choosing a goalkeeper for your school team. First you write down what a goalkeeper must do. Then you write down the kind of player you want. Then players tell you about themselves, and you compare them. Recruitment means finding and attracting people to apply for a job. Selection means choosing between them. Four documents help a business do both well.

Method. A job description is about the job: its title, tasks, duties and responsibilities. A person specification is about the person: the qualifications, skills, experience and qualities the ideal applicant should have. The business writes both. An application form is designed by the business and filled in by the applicant, so everyone answers the same questions. A CV, or curriculum vitae, is written by the applicant. It summarises their education, qualifications, work experience and referees. So first ask who writes the document. Then say what it contains, how that helps match the right person to the job, and the result for the business.

Worked example. Here is a real question from Paper two in November twenty twenty-five. Analyse how recruitment documents may help Samsung to hire the best employees. It is worth six marks, so it needs two developed chains, both about Samsung. First, pull out the context. Samsung designs phones and televisions, employs more than two hundred and seventy thousand people, and competes with Apple and Xiaomi. It needs skilled engineers. Chain one. The person specification sets out the qualifications Samsung needs, such as an engineering degree and design experience. People without these are less likely to apply, so HR spends less time on weak applications. Chain two. Application forms ask everyone the same questions, so Samsung can compare each person's design experience fairly against the job description. It hires the most skilled engineers, who design innovative products to compete with Apple. This explanation is by olevels.com, not an official mark scheme.

Common mistake. A common mistake is mixing up the two business documents. The job description describes the job. The person specification describes the person. Another mistake is getting the writer wrong. The applicant writes the CV. The business writes the job description and person specification.

Your turn. Now you try. A new hospital needs nurses. Which document would list a nursing qualification and two years of experience? Who writes it, and how does it help the hospital? Pause and decide.

Answer. It is the person specification, written by the hospital. Unqualified people can see they are unsuitable and are less likely to apply, so shortlisting is quicker. The hospital hires qualified nurses who need less training and care for patients safely. Remember the method: say who writes the document, what it contains, and how that helps the business hire the right person. Now try the practice questions.
