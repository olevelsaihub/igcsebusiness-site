Idea. Imagine you sell cupcakes at a school fair for two dollars each, and you sell forty. You receive two times forty, which is eighty dollars. That money is your revenue. Pay works the same way. Remuneration is the total reward paid to employees. If you earn ten dollars an hour for six hours, your pay is sixty dollars.

Method. Step one, find the rate: the price per item, or the pay per hour. For revenue, always use the selling price, not a cost. Step two, find every quantity. Watch the time period. Five sales every month for a year means five times twelve. If there are several shops or factories, add them together first. Step three, multiply the rate by the total quantity. Step four, write the currency and round as asked.

Worked example. Here is a real question from Paper one in November 2024, about a robot maker in Türkiye. Each battery pack sells for ninety dollars, and it sold five every month in 2023. Calculate the total revenue for the year. The rate is ninety dollars per pack. In one month, revenue is ninety times five, which is four hundred and fifty dollars. There are twelve months in a year. Four hundred and fifty times twelve is five thousand four hundred dollars. Or, sixty packs times ninety dollars gives the same.

Common mistake. The most common mistake is stopping at one month. Four hundred and fifty dollars is only one month. The question asked for the whole year. Another mistake is multiplying only one group. If two shops each sell goods, add both shops' sales before you multiply by the price.

Your turn. Now you try. A café employs three workers. Each works twenty-five hours a week at eight dollars fifty an hour. Calculate the café's total weekly pay. Pause here and work it out.

Answer. First, the total quantity. Three workers each work twenty-five hours, so that is seventy-five hours a week. Now multiply by the rate. Seventy-five times eight dollars fifty is six hundred and thirty-seven dollars fifty a week. Remember the method: find the rate, find every quantity including the time period, then multiply.
