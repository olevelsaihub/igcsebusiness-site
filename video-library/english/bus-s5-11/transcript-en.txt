Idea. Think of a car factory. A robot arm paints every car door with exactly the same movement, all day and all night, without a break. That is robotics at work. Robotics means using robots, which are programmed machines, to carry out production tasks instead of people. Technology usually raises productivity: the output per worker. Ten workers making two hundred units is twenty each. With robots, four workers making four hundred units is one hundred each.

Method. Robots bring four main gains. Fewer errors, because they repeat tasks identically. Faster output. Working twenty-four hours a day. And lower labour costs. Next, explain how the robot causes that gain, then end with the result for the business: better quality, higher productivity, lower cost per unit, or higher profit. When you are asked to judge, weigh the gains against the drawbacks: robots are expensive to buy and maintain, workers may lose their jobs, and robots are less flexible than people for one-off tasks. Which side is heavier depends on the business, so say why for that firm.

Worked example. Here is a real exam question from Paper two in November 2024. Explain one reason why a business would use robotics in its production. It is worth three marks. Mark one is the reason. Using robots reduces mistakes in production. Mark two explains how. A robot is programmed to make exactly the same movement every time, and it does not get tired or bored like a person might. Mark three gives the result. Every product is made to the same standard, so quality is more consistent and fewer faulty items are thrown away. This explanation is by olevels.com, not an official mark scheme.

Common mistake. A common mistake is saying robots are better or more efficient, without saying how, or what the result is. Another mistake is calling robots cheap. They can lower the cost of each unit over time, but they cost a lot to buy and maintain.

Your turn. Now you try. A toy factory replaces hand painting with a robot arm that works day and night. Explain one benefit for the factory, then give one drawback. Pause and work it out.

Answer. The robot works day and night without breaks, so more toys are painted each day. Higher output lowers the cost of each toy. One drawback: the robot arm is expensive to buy, and painters may lose their jobs. Remember the method: name one gain, explain how the robot causes it, then end with the result for the business.
