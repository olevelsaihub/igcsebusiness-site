Idea. Imagine a café owner who has an idea for a new drink. She makes hundreds of them without asking anyone. Nobody wants it, and her money is wasted. This type asks why a business does market research. The purpose is the reason for the research, not the method used to collect it.

Method. Step one: name one purpose. Research helps a business understand customer needs and wants. It can reveal a gap in the market, which is a need that no business is meeting yet. And it reduces risk, because the business is less likely to spend money on a product nobody wants. Step two: say what the business does with the findings. They help it decide on the product, its price, where it is sold and how it is promoted. Step three: finish with a business result, such as more sales or less chance of failure. And always name the business's own product and customers. In longer evaluate answers, weigh both sides. Research lowers risk, but it costs money, takes time, and gives no guarantee of success.

Worked example. Here is a real question from Paper one in November 2020. Explain one purpose of market research for a business. It is worth three marks: one purpose, then two linked steps. Mark one is the purpose: to identify what customers need and want. Mark two is the action. Knowing this, the business can design new products, or change existing ones, to match what customers want. Mark three is the result. Customers are more likely to buy, so sales rise and there is less risk of wasting money on products that do not sell.

Common mistake. Mistake one: giving a method when the question asks for a purpose. Carrying out a survey is how, not why. Mistake two: stopping at the purpose. Carry the chain on to what the business does, and then the result.

Your turn. Now you try. A gym is thinking of opening at five in the morning. It asks its members whether they would come that early. Explain one purpose of this research for the gym. Pause here and work it out.

Answer. One purpose is to reduce risk. The gym finds out whether enough members want early opening, and only opens early if they do. So it avoids paying staff and energy bills for empty hours. That is the pattern for this type: name a purpose, say what the business does with the findings, then give the result. Now try the practice questions.
