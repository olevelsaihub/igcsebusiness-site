Primary, secondary and tertiary sectors

Idea. Think about a cotton T-shirt. Before you can buy it, a farmer grows the cotton, a factory makes the T-shirt, and a shop sells it to you. Each kind of work belongs to a sector of the economy. There are three sectors: primary, secondary and tertiary. The primary sector takes raw materials from the earth or the sea. Farming, fishing, mining and forestry are all primary. The secondary sector turns raw materials into finished or part-finished goods. Manufacturing, building and engineering are secondary. The tertiary sector provides services. Shops, banks, insurance, hotels, restaurants and transport are all tertiary.

Method. Step one: ask what the business actually does. Does it extract, make, or provide a service? The activity decides the sector, not the product. Here is the classic trap. A supermarket sells goods, but it does not make them. Selling is a service, so a shop is tertiary. Step two: match it. Extract is primary, make is secondary, serve is tertiary. Step three: in an outline question, give the reason, then add a detail from the case.

Worked example. Here is a real question from Paper one in November 2023. AWG is a clothing business in Iceland. Outline why AWG can be classified as operating in the secondary sector. It is worth two marks. Mark one is the reason. The secondary sector makes goods from raw materials, and AWG manufactures goods. Mark two applies it to AWG. In its own workshops, it turns natural wool into jumpers, dresses and baby clothes. This explanation is by olevels.com, not an official mark scheme.

Common mistake. The most common mistake is putting shops in the secondary sector because they sell goods. Shops sell, they do not make, so they are tertiary. In an outline, a general reason with no detail from the case earns only one mark. Name the real materials and products.

Your turn. Now you try. Which sector is each of these in: a fishing boat crew, a car factory, and a taxi company? Pause and decide.

Answer. The fishing crew takes fish from the sea, so it is primary. The car factory makes goods, so it is secondary. The taxi company provides transport, which is a service, so it is tertiary. Remember the method: ask what the business does, match it to extract, make or serve, then name the sector. Now try the practice questions for this type.
