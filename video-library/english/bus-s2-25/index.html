Financial and non-financial methods of motivation

Why would a business care whether its workers are motivated? Because motivated employees work harder, make fewer mistakes, and are less likely to leave. Businesses use two kinds of method. Financial methods involve money. Non-financial methods do not involve extra money; they change the job itself.

The financial methods are remuneration, which is the pay for the work, a bonus, commission, promotion, and fringe benefits. A bonus is an extra payment for reaching a target. Commission is pay based on how much an employee sells. So a bonus rewards a target, and commission rewards each sale. Fringe benefits are extras on top of normal pay, such as staff discounts, a company car, free gym use or health insurance. The non-financial methods are job rotation, job enrichment, and autonomy. Job rotation means moving between different jobs, for example reception one month and the restaurant the next. Job enrichment means harder tasks and more responsibility. Autonomy means freedom to decide how you do your own work. To explain any method, use one chain: name the method, say how it affects the employees, then link it to the business: its productivity, sales, staff turnover, or costs.

Here is a real question from Paper 1 in June 2025. Explain one impact to a business of providing fringe benefits to employees. It is worth three marks: one impact, then two linked steps of explanation. Mark one is the impact. Fringe benefits make employees more loyal, so fewer of them leave. Staff turnover falls. Mark two explains why. Employees value extras that other employers may not offer, so they are less likely to move to a competitor. Mark three gives the consequence for the business. It spends less time and money recruiting and training replacements, which keeps its costs down.

Mistake one: calling fringe benefits or promotion non-financial. Both involve money, so both are financial. Mistake two: in longer justify answers, ignoring cost. Financial methods cost money, and some staff may not want more responsibility. Weigh this for the business in the question.

Now you try. A clothing shop wants each sales assistant to sell more. Which method links each worker's pay directly to their own sales: a bonus, commission, or job rotation? Then think of one reason it helps the shop. Pause and decide.

The answer is commission. Each assistant earns more for every sale, so they try harder to sell to each customer, which raises the shop's sales revenue. That is the pattern for every answer on this type: name the method, explain its effect on the employees, then link it to the business. Now try the practice questions.
