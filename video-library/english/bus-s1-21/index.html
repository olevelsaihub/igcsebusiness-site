Trading blocs and tariffs

Idea. Imagine a pair of trainers made in another country. When they arrive at the border, the government may add a tax before they can be sold. That tax is called a tariff. Say the trainers cost one hundred dollars and the tariff is ten per cent. Ten per cent of one hundred is ten dollars, so they now cost one hundred and ten dollars. A tariff makes imports dearer. A trading bloc is a group of countries that agree to remove trade barriers, such as tariffs, between themselves. Members trade freely, but often keep tariffs on goods from countries outside the bloc.

Method. Step one. Recall the two ideas: a bloc removes barriers between members, and a tariff is a tax on imports. Step two. Decide whose side the question asks about: the country, a business inside the bloc, or an importer. Step three. Link the feature to prices or costs. Step four. Finish with the outcome: sales, choice, profit or jobs.

Worked example. Here is a real question from Paper two in November 2024. Explain one advantage for a country of being in a trading bloc. It is worth three marks. Mark one is the advantage. The country's businesses can sell to other members without paying tariffs. Mark two is the link. With no tariff, the goods carry no extra tax cost, so the country's exports stay cheaper in the other member countries. Mark three is the consequence. Its businesses sell more, which raises output and creates more jobs in the country.

Common mistake. Mistake one: saying tariffs make imports cheaper. A tariff is a tax, so it makes imports dearer. Mistake two: defining a trading bloc when the question asks for an advantage. The definition earns no marks there. Go straight to the advantage and explain it.

Your turn. Now you try. Sunny Fruits grows mangoes in a country that has just joined a trading bloc. Explain one advantage to Sunny Fruits of its country joining. Pause and decide.

Answer. One advantage is a bigger market. Sunny Fruits can sell mangoes to every member country with no tariffs, so its mangoes are not made dearer abroad. It can sell more, which raises its revenue and profit. Remember the method: recall bloc and tariff, decide whose side, link to prices or costs, then finish with the outcome.
