Idea. Think about your own money. On Monday you have twenty dollars. During the week you earn fifteen dollars and spend twelve. Money in minus money out is fifteen minus twelve, which is three dollars. This is the net cash flow. Add it to what you started with. Twenty plus three is twenty-three dollars. This is the closing balance, and it becomes next week's opening balance.

Method. Step one: if there is more than one line, add up all the inflows and all the outflows. Step two: net cash flow is inflows minus outflows. Five thousand minus five thousand five hundred is minus five hundred. Keep the minus sign. Step three: if the closing balance is asked for, add net cash flow to the opening balance. Two thousand plus minus five hundred is one thousand five hundred dollars. Step four: if a figure is missing, rearrange. Outflows equal inflows minus net cash flow. Step five: check whether the question wants net cash flow or the closing balance.

Worked example. Here is a real exam question from June 2024, Paper one. Using Figure three, calculate the closing balance. It is worth two marks. Figure three, in euros, shows an opening balance of sixteen thousand, a cash inflow of twenty-two thousand, and a cash outflow of twenty thousand. Net cash flow is twenty-two thousand minus twenty thousand, which is two thousand. Then the closing balance. Sixteen thousand plus two thousand is eighteen thousand euros.

Common mistake. A common mistake is adding the inflow and outflow together. Net cash flow is a subtraction. Another is giving the wrong line. If the question asks for net cash flow, do not give the closing balance, or the other way round.

Your turn. Now you try. A café has an opening balance of three thousand dollars. In May its cash inflows are seven thousand four hundred and its cash outflows are nine thousand one hundred. Calculate the net cash flow and the closing balance. Pause here and work it out.

Answer. Net cash flow is seven thousand four hundred minus nine thousand one hundred, which is minus one thousand seven hundred dollars. Closing balance is three thousand plus minus one thousand seven hundred, which is one thousand three hundred dollars. That becomes June's opening balance. Remember the method: total the inflows and outflows, net cash flow is in minus out, and closing balance is opening plus net. Now try the practice questions.
