Idea. Have you ever searched online for football boots, and then seen adverts for boots everywhere the next day? That is not luck. The advert was chosen for you. This is targeted online advertising. It shows adverts only to people likely to be interested, based on their searches, purchases or profile.

Method. Step one: name the digital tool. Besides targeted adverts, there is social media; viral adverts, which people share quickly with each other; e-newsletters, which a business sends electronically to its customers; and the business's own website. Step two: say what makes it useful. Digital promotion can reach people worldwide, at any time, often more cheaply than traditional methods, and it can be precisely targeted. Step three: link the tool to sales, repeat purchases or loyalty. So the chain is: the tool, why it is useful, then the result. Step four: social media has two sides. Good reviews spread quickly and attract customers. But bad or untrue reviews can drive customers away, and not everyone uses social media.

Worked example. Here is a real question from Paper two R in June 2019. Explain one advantage for a business of using targeted advertising online. It is worth three marks. Mark one is the advantage. The adverts reach the people most likely to be interested, such as those who recently searched for similar products. Mark two explains why that matters. Fewer adverts are wasted on people who would never buy, so the business gets better value than with many traditional methods. Mark three is the result. More of the people who see the advert go on to buy, so sales increase.

Common mistake. Mistake one: saying online advertising is good with no reason. Give the reason, such as targeting or low cost. Mistake two: defining an e-newsletter when the question asks for a benefit.

Your turn. Now you try. A small cake shop has two thousand followers on social media. Explain one disadvantage of social media for the shop. Pause here and work it out.

Answer. One unhappy customer may post a bad review, or even an untrue one. Many of the two thousand followers could see it quickly. Some may then buy their cakes elsewhere, so the shop's sales fall. That is the method for this type: name the digital tool, say why it is useful or risky, then link it to sales. Now try the practice questions.
