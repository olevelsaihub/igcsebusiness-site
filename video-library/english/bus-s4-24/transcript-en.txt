Idea. Think about bottled water. It is sold in almost every shop, to almost everyone. Now think about a shop that sells only left-handed scissors and tools. It serves a small, special group. Selling the same product to the whole market, aiming for as many customers as possible, is mass marketing. Targeting a small, specialised segment with a product made for it is niche marketing.

Method. First decide which approach the business uses. Mass marketing reaches huge numbers of people, so sales can be very high. Producing on a large scale lowers the cost of each unit. These are economies of scale. But mass marketing needs big promotion budgets and faces many competitors. A niche business can charge higher prices and build strong loyalty, because there are fewer competitors. But the market is small, so total sales are limited and the business depends on one group. Then pick one benefit, link it to the customers, how many are reached or how well the product fits them, and end with the result: revenue, profit or spread of risk.

Worked example. Here is a real question from Paper two in November 2025. Explain one advantage to a business of mass marketing. It is worth three marks, and no definition is needed. Mark one is the advantage. Mass marketing can generate a very high volume of sales. Mark two explains why. The same product is promoted to the whole market, so a very large number of people know about it and may buy it. Mark three gives the result. Large sales raise revenue, and producing on a large scale lowers the cost per unit, so profit improves.

Common mistake. Mistake one: thinking mass marketing just means lots of advertising. It means aiming one product at the whole market. Mistake two: writing a definition in an Explain answer. A definition earns nothing there. Give the advantage and follow it through to sales or profit.

Your turn. Now you try. A small bakery makes gluten-free birthday cakes for people who cannot eat wheat. Is this mass or niche marketing? Explain one advantage of this approach. Pause and decide.

Answer. It is niche marketing: a small, specialised group with a product made for it. These customers have few other choices, so they stay loyal and will pay a higher price. That means more profit on each cake. That is the pattern for every answer on this type: decide mass or niche, link one advantage to the customers, then end with revenue, profit or risk.
