Imagine your pocket money goes up from twenty dollars a week to twenty-five dollars. How much has it changed? Business questions ask this all the time, about sales, revenue and customers. The absolute change is the new value minus the old value. Twenty-five minus twenty is five dollars. It stays in the original unit. The percentage change compares the change with where you started. Five out of the original twenty is a quarter, so twenty-five per cent.

Step one. Find the old value and the new value. Check you are using the right row and the right years, because tables often show more than you need. Step two, subtract: new minus old. Step three, for a percentage, divide by the old value and multiply by one hundred. Step four, round as asked and say increase or decrease.

Here is a real question from Paper two R in November 2020. A sports equipment maker's revenue rose from eighty-five thousand and eighty-three to ninety-two thousand three hundred and twenty-two, in thousands of rupees. Calculate the percentage increase to two decimal places. The old value is eighty-five thousand and eighty-three. The new value is ninety-two thousand three hundred and twenty-two. Subtract: the change is seven thousand two hundred and thirty-nine. Now divide by the old value and multiply by one hundred. That gives eight point five zero eight, which rounds to eight point five one per cent. It is an increase.

The most common mistake is dividing by the new value. That gives seven point eight four per cent, which is wrong. Always divide by the old value. Another mistake is dividing new by old without subtracting first. That gives one hundred and eight point five one per cent. Subtract first, then divide.

Now you try. A bakery sold six hundred and forty loaves in May and five hundred and fifty-two loaves in June. Calculate the percentage change, to two decimal places. Pause here and work it out.

May is the old value. Five hundred and fifty-two minus six hundred and forty is minus eighty-eight. The minus sign tells us sales fell. Eighty-eight divided by six hundred and forty, times one hundred, is thirteen point seven five. So sales fell by thirteen point seven five per cent. Remember the method: find the change, divide by the old value, multiply by one hundred, then round and say increase or decrease. Now try the practice questions.
