Imagine two bakeries in your town. One sponsors the school football team and gives its unsold bread to a food bank. Soon, people start speaking well of it. Public relations, or PR, means building and keeping a good image of a business and its products in the eyes of the public. It includes charity donations, community projects, sponsoring events, environmental actions and press releases.

Step one: name how the business shapes its image, using the case. Step two: link that to how people see the business. They trust it, respect it, and see it as caring. Step three: carry it to a business result. More customers choose it, so sales and profit rise. A respected business can also attract investors and staff. Step four: note the other side. PR costs money, and there is no guarantee it will bring more sales. Bad publicity, such as faulty products or harm to the environment, can undo it.

Here is a real question from Paper two in November 2021. Explain one reason why having good public relations is important to a business. It is worth three marks, and no definition is needed. Mark one is the reason. Good public relations improves the image the public has of the business. Mark two is the link. If the business is in the news for supporting a local charity or cutting waste, people see it as caring and trustworthy. Mark three is the result. Customers are more willing to choose its products over competitors', so sales and profit can rise.

Mistake one: describing PR as advertising or selling. PR is about managing the business's image. Mistake two: stopping at improves image. Carry it through to how customers act, and then to sales or profit.

Now you try. A clothing company starts collecting old clothes from its customers to recycle them, and the local news reports it. Explain one reason why this good PR could help the company. Pause and decide.

One good answer: the news story shows the company cares about the environment. Customers who care about waste trust it more, so they choose its clothes over rivals' and sales rise. That is the pattern for every answer on this type: the PR action, the better image, then the result for sales or profit. Now try the practice questions.
