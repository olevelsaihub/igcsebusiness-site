Idea. Imagine a café near your school. It sells coffee and sandwiches, just like any other café. But it trains young people who could not find a job, and it uses its profit to pay for more training places. A business like this is called a social enterprise. It sells goods or services, but its main aim is to help people or the environment. What happens to the profit? Most of it goes back into the social purpose or the community, rather than being paid out to the owners.

Method. Step one. Say that it is a business that trades. It sells goods or services, so it is not just a charity collecting donations. Step two. Add its social purpose, and say how the profit is used to benefit people, the community or the environment. Step three. If a State question asks why an owner set one up, give one reason that fits the owner in the case, such as wanting to help their local community while running a business.

Worked example. Here is a real question from Paper two in November 2020. Define the term social enterprise. It is worth one mark. Start by saying it is a business that sells goods or services. Then add the purpose. Its main aim is to help society or the environment, so most of its profit is used for that purpose, such as supporting the local community, rather than being paid to its owners.

Common mistake. Mistake one: saying a social enterprise makes no profit. It can make a profit. It just uses most of it for its purpose. Mistake two: leaving out the social purpose. A business that sells goods to make money fits every business. Always add who or what it helps.

Your turn. Now you try. Amira runs a shop that sells bags made from old fishing nets. She uses most of the profit to clean up local beaches. State one reason why Amira set up her business as a social enterprise. Pause and decide.

Answer. One reason: Amira wants to protect the local environment by cleaning up the beaches, while still running a business that pays its own way. Remember the method: it is a business that trades, its main aim is to help people or the environment, and its profit is used for that aim. Now try the practice questions.
