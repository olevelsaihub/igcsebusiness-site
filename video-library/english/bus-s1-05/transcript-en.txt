Idea. Think about your local bakery. The owner wants profit. The bakers want fair pay. Customers want fresh bread at a good price. Neighbours want no noise or mess. All of these people care about what the bakery does. Each of these people or groups is called a stakeholder. A stakeholder is any person or group that is affected by, or has an interest in, what a business does. Now think about a large company. It is split into small pieces of ownership called shares. Anyone who owns one or more shares in a company is a shareholder, so they are a part-owner. So every shareholder is a stakeholder, because owners care about the business. But not every stakeholder is a shareholder. A customer cares about the business but does not own it.

Method. Step one: decide which term the question asks for. Stakeholder and shareholder look alike, so read the word carefully. Step two: for stakeholder, give the meaning: a person or group with an interest in, or affected by, the business. The interest link earns the mark. Step three: for shareholder, say it is a person or organisation that owns shares in a company. The word own is what earns the mark. Step four: you may add one short example, but the meaning always comes first. An example on its own is not a definition.

Worked example. Here is a real question from Paper one in November 2020. Define the term stakeholder. It is worth one mark. The command is define, so give the meaning in one sentence. A stakeholder is any person or group that is affected by, or has an interest in, the activities of a business. Then add a short example to show you understand, such as its employees, customers or the local community. This explanation is by olevels.com, not an official mark scheme.

Common mistake. The most common mistake is saying a stakeholder is someone who owns the business. That describes a shareholder or an owner. Another mistake is being vague. A shareholder is not just someone who invests. Say clearly that they own shares in a company.

Your turn. Now you try. Define the term shareholder. Then decide: is a business that supplies plastic to a toy company, and owns no shares in it, a stakeholder, a shareholder, or both? Pause and decide.

Answer. A shareholder is a person or organisation that owns one or more shares in a company. The supplier is a stakeholder, because the toy company's orders affect its sales. It is not a shareholder, because it owns no shares. Remember the method: decide which term is asked, give its meaning in one sentence, then add one short example.
