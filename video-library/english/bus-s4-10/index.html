Think of a shop you always go back to. It sells what you like, and the staff are friendly. So you return, and you tell your friends about it. That is what marketing is about. Marketing means identifying what customers want, and making sure the business provides it at a profit.

A market orientated business finds out what customers want first, then makes it. A product orientated business makes what it is good at, then tries to sell it. Customer loyalty means customers keep buying from the same business instead of its rivals. Loyalty cards and special offers encourage it. Poor customer service does the opposite and drives customers away. Market share is the percentage of all sales in a market made by one business. If a town's phone case sales are split forty, thirty-five and twenty-five per cent, brand A has the largest share. To explain any benefit of marketing, use one chain. Name the benefit, explain how customers behave, then give the result for the business: revenue, market share or lower costs.

Here is a real question from Paper two R in June 2019. Explain one advantage for a business of having loyal customers. It is worth three marks. Mark one is the advantage: repeat custom. Mark two explains how customers behave. Loyal customers trust the business, so they keep coming back instead of shopping with competitors. Mark three gives the result. The business gets a steady flow of sales, which increases its revenue and can increase its market share.

Mistake one: stopping at happy customers. Carry the chain on to sales, revenue or market share. Mistake two: mixing up the orientations. Market orientation starts with what customers want.

Now you try. A hair salon gives each customer a card, and every tenth haircut is free. Explain one advantage of this loyalty card to the salon. Pause here and work it out.

The advantage is repeat custom. Customers want to reach the free tenth haircut, so they keep coming back instead of trying rival salons. This gives the salon steady sales and higher revenue. That is the pattern for this type: name the benefit, explain how customers behave, then give the result for the business. Now try the practice questions.
