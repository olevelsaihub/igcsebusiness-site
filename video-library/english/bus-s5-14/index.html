Imagine you buy headphones and they break after a week. Would you buy that brand again? Probably not, and you might warn your friends too. Quality means a good or service meets the standard customers expect and does its job well. For a service, that means helpful staff, good facilities, and doing what was promised.

There are two ways to manage quality. Quality control checks products, often at the end of production, so faulty items are found before customers get them. Total quality management, or TQM, makes every employee at every stage responsible for quality, supported by training and teamwork. Next, build the chain. Start with what quality changes, such as fewer faults or satisfied customers. Link that to repeat customers and recommendations. Then end with sales, profit, or the ability to charge higher prices. Quality can give a competitive advantage: a reason customers choose one business over its rivals. But quality also costs money. Inspections, training and better materials all add cost and take time. Which side is heavier depends on the business.

Here is a real exam question from Paper two in November 2023. Explain one reason why the quality of goods made by a business is important to its success. It is worth three marks. Mark one is the reason. High-quality goods give a business a good reputation. Mark two links it. Pleased customers buy again and recommend the business to friends, instead of switching to a competitor. Mark three gives the result. The business gains more customers and higher sales, which helps it make more profit and succeed. This explanation is by olevels.com, not an official mark scheme.

A common mistake is stopping at customers will be happy. Keep going: link it to repeat sales, reputation or profit. Another mistake is mixing up quality control and TQM. Quality control is checking, often at the end. TQM makes everyone responsible, all the way through.

Now you try. At a laptop maker, every worker checks their own work at each stage, and all staff are trained in quality. Is this quality control or TQM? Give one benefit for the business. Pause and decide.

It is TQM, because every worker is responsible for quality at every stage. Faults are caught early, so fewer faulty laptops are returned, repair costs fall, and the brand's reputation grows. Remember the method: say what quality changes, link it to customers, then end with sales, profit or reputation for the business. Now try the practice questions.
