Imagine thirty students vote for their lunch. Twelve choose pizza, ten choose pasta and eight choose salad. What share of the class chose pizza? Divide the part by the total, then multiply by one hundred. Twelve divided by thirty is zero point four. Times one hundred gives forty per cent. Businesses use the same idea. Market share is one business's sales as a percentage of the total sales of all the businesses in the market.

Step one: find the part. Read the question carefully and pick the figure for the business or place it names. Here, we want Alpha. Step two: find the total. If it is not given, add up all the parts. Check they are in the same unit, here millions of pounds. Fifty plus thirty plus forty is one hundred and twenty. Step three: divide the part by the total and multiply by one hundred. Fifty divided by one hundred and twenty, times one hundred, is forty-one point six six recurring. Step four: round as asked, usually to two decimal places, giving forty-one point six seven per cent.

Here is a real question from Paper two R in June 2024. Calculate, to two decimal places, the percentage of Jimmy Choo shops in the UK. It is worth two marks. The figure shows the shops in each country. Canada has five, the UK has seven, Ireland has two and Australia has one. The UK is our part. Add all the parts. Five plus seven plus two plus one is fifteen shops. This earns the first mark. Now divide. Seven divided by fifteen, times one hundred, is forty-six point six six recurring. To two decimal places, that is forty-six point six seven per cent.

A common mistake is dividing the wrong way round. Fifteen divided by seven gives over two hundred per cent. A share of a total can never be more than one hundred per cent. Another mistake is leaving out a part. Forgetting Australia gives a total of fourteen, and a wrong answer of fifty per cent.

Now you try. Three cafés sell coffee in a town. Weekly sales are four thousand dollars for Bean Box, two thousand five hundred for Cup Corner, and one thousand five hundred for Daily Brew. Calculate Cup Corner's market share to two decimal places. Pause here and work it out.

First the total. Four thousand plus two thousand five hundred plus one thousand five hundred is eight thousand dollars. Cup Corner is the part. Two thousand five hundred divided by eight thousand, times one hundred, is thirty-one point two five per cent. Remember the method: find the part, find the total, then divide the part by the total and multiply by one hundred. Now try the practice questions for this type.
