Why does a brand new phone often launch at a very high price, while a new snack is sometimes sold cheaply at first? Each business has chosen a different way to set its price. The method a business uses to decide its price is called a pricing strategy. There are five to know: cost plus, penetration, skimming, competition pricing and promotional pricing.

Cost plus: work out the cost of making the product, then add a markup for profit. If a candle costs four dollars to make and the markup is two dollars, the price is six dollars. Penetration means a low price for a new product, to gain market share quickly, then raise it later. Skimming is the opposite: a high price for a new or unique product, then lower it over time. Competition pricing sets a price similar to rivals. Promotional pricing lowers the price for a short time to boost sales, such as a discount or special offer. Next, link the price to sales and profit. A low price usually brings more sales, but less profit on each one. A high price brings fewer sales, but more profit on each one. So the method is: name the strategy, match it to the situation, such as a new product or many rivals, then explain the effect on sales and profit.

Here is a real question from Paper two R in November 2020. Explain one benefit of using penetration pricing for a new product. It is worth three marks. Mark one is the benefit: the new product becomes established in the market quickly. Mark two explains why. The price is lower than competitors', so customers are tempted to try it instead of their usual brands, and it gains market share fast. Mark three is the result. Once customers are used to buying it, the business can raise the price gradually and earn more profit on each sale, while keeping many of them.

The most common mistake is swapping skimming and penetration. Skimming starts high. Penetration starts low.

Now you try. A technology firm launches the first folding laptop. No rival sells one yet. Should it use skimming or penetration pricing? Give one reason. Pause and decide.

Skimming. The laptop is unique, so keen early buyers will pay a high price. The high profit on each sale helps pay back the cost of developing it. The price can be lowered later, when rivals arrive. That is the method for this type: name the strategy, match it to the situation, then explain the effect on sales and profit. Now try the practice questions.
