Idea. Before a football season, a team sets a target: win the league, or just avoid relegation. A business does the same. Its targets are called objectives. Objectives give the owner and staff a clear direction, motivate them, and give a target to check progress against. Financial objectives are about money: survival, profit, sales, market share, and financial security. Non-financial objectives are not mainly about money: social objectives, personal satisfaction, challenge, independence and control. A social objective aims to help society or the environment, such as cutting waste. Objectives also change as a business evolves: because of market conditions, new technology, its own results, new laws, or growth and new owners.

Method. Step one: decide whether the objective is financial or non-financial. Watch out: survival and market share are both financial. Step two: find what the owner or business in the case actually wants, using a detail from the case. Step three: in an explain answer, give one point, then ask so what, twice. That gives one point and two linked steps.

Worked example. Here is a real question from Paper two in November 2025, about Samsung. Explain one advantage to a business of having a social objective. It is worth three marks. Mark one is the advantage. A social objective can improve the business's reputation. Mark two explains why. When Samsung is seen to help society, customers are more likely to trust and like the brand. Mark three gives the consequence. More people may choose its phones and televisions instead of rivals such as Apple, so its sales rise.

Common mistake. Mistake one: calling survival or market share non-financial. Both are financial objectives. Mistake two: defining social objective when the question asks for an advantage. A definition is not an advantage, so it earns no marks here.

Your turn. Now you try. Maya opens a small bakery. She wants to stay open through her first year, and she gives leftover bread to a local shelter every evening. Which objective is financial, and which is non-financial? Pause and decide.

Answer. Staying open through her first year is survival, a financial objective. Giving bread to the shelter helps the community, so it is a social objective, which is non-financial. Remember the method: sort the objective, fit it to the business in the case, then build one point with two linked steps. Now try the practice questions for this type.
