Branding and why it matters

Idea. Picture a shelf full of trainers that look almost the same. You still spot your favourite in a second, just from its name, logo or colours. A brand is a name, logo, symbol or design that identifies a business's products and makes them different from competitors' products.

Method. Why does branding matter? Customers recognise the product quickly. They become loyal and keep choosing it. They trust its quality, so the business can charge more. New products are easier to launch, and the brand itself becomes valuable. Step one: say what makes the brand recognisable. Step two: choose one reason it matters. Step three: chain that reason to how customers behave, and then to sales, price or survival. Step four: use the case. Mention the business's products, the rivals nearby, and how long it has been trading.

Worked example. Here is a real question from Paper two R in June 2023. Explain one reason why branding is important to businesses. It is worth three marks. You do not need a definition. Mark one is the reason. Branding makes a business's products stand out from those of its competitors. Mark two is the link. Customers quickly recognise the name or logo and know what quality to expect, so they can easily find the brand they want. Mark three is the result. Over time, customers keep choosing it rather than trying rivals. This builds loyalty and brings more repeat sales.

Common mistake. Mistake one: thinking branding is just advertising. A brand is the name, logo or design that identifies the product. Advertising is promotion, and public relations is about the business's image. Mistake two: saying branding makes a product popular, and stopping. Follow it through to customers and then to sales or price.

Your turn. Now you try. A new gym opens in a town that already has three rival gyms. Explain one reason why a strong brand would help it. Pause and decide.

Answer. One good answer: a clear name and logo make the new gym stand out from its three rivals. People notice it and remember it when they choose a gym, so more of them join and revenue rises. That is the pattern for every answer on this type: one reason, its effect on customers, then the result for sales, price or survival. Now try the practice questions.
